## 3. Major Findings and Novel Contributions

### 3.1 The Correction Trajectory as Evidence

The single most important contribution of Section II is the methodology evolution chain (Stratum B). The trajectory of corrections -- not the absence of error -- is the evidence that the methodology works. Each correction came from outside the producer, demonstrating Axiom 5 (evidence independence) operating on the methodology itself. ### 3.2 The Format Constraint Fallacy

The Crystallographic Ijaz v0.2 (R0042) identifies a fundamental failure mode: trying to solve a generator problem with an output format rule. The count-list coupling constraint verified syntax (the count matched the list beside it) but not semantics (the list contained the right items). The ASSERTED escape valve became confession rather than treatment. Resolution: replace the generator (LLM-authored register -> code-generated register).

This finding has implications far beyond the XZ Protocol: it applies to any system where LLMs are asked to self-verify through structural constraints.

Across all methodology versions, the empirical result is stable: Class A defects persist with Hurst exponent H=1.0. The interpretation is the contribution: this is the heartbeat of a live verification system. The instrument cannot verify that it has no remaining byte-false claims about its own verification properties. The destination is not zero defects; the destination is disclosure.

### 3.4 Publication Posture and Self-Referential Honesty

The prohibition on effectiveness verbs (R0061) is a structural honesty discipline applied to the program's own marketing. The Unified Structural Audit Methodology (R0076) decomposes every audit into five questions answered by five models: WHERE (Fractal Cube), WHAT (Enzyme-Substrate), HOW (Replication Fork), WHO (Quantum Orbital), WHEN (Cell Cycle Checkpoint). ### 3.7 Rice's Theorem Correction

The Differentiator v6.1 (R0044) corrects v5.0's use of Godel's Incompleteness where Rice's theorem is the appropriate result.
