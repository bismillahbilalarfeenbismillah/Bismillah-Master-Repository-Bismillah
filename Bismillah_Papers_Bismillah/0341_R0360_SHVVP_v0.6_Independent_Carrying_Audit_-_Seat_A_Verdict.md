# SHVVP v0.6 Independent Carrying Audit - Seat A Verdict

**Auditor:** GPT-class / OpenAI-family independent carrying seat

**Carrying decision:** **I do not carry SHVVP v0.6 for deposit or convergence.**

## 1. Executive determination

The packet is authentic to every supplied outer and nested SHA-256 receipt. The
fixed-denominator and omission portions of F-P5-1 are materially improved: R4-C is
committed to 216, an unreconciled `30/30` subset is rejected, a reconciled shortfall
is scored over all 216 items, an omitted committed component becomes `INCOMPLETE`,
and the single predeclared R3 exclusion still becomes `OUT OF SCOPE`. The flagship
PDF is 56 pages, structurally openable, free of all-black pages, and its reflowed
Section 8.2 tables are legible.

The release nevertheless **does not pass**. For every `SEALED` and `REALIZED`
universe, the normative gate merely checks that a caller supplied a non-empty hash
and a caller-supplied cardinality. It never compares either value with an
independently frozen pre-results commitment. A caller can therefore select a
favourable subset after seeing results, declare that subset to be the entire sealed
or realized universe, add any non-empty string as the purported seal or run-log
hash, and obtain `SUPPORTED-DESCRIPTIVE`. I executed this attack against R1, R2 and
R5. It succeeds.

This is the same class of post-result evidence-universe selection that F-P5-1
required the successor to close. Because it remains executable in the normative
route gate, it is **HIGH and BLOCKING**. The source also retains multiple live v0.5
status statements, including a table saying the round-5 verdicts are pending and an
ending that calls the current document the frozen v0.5 contract. That separately
fails the mandatory F-P5-2 closure check.

## 4. Findings

## F-P6-1

- **ID:** F-P6-1
- **SEVERITY:** **HIGH - BLOCKING**
- **LOCUS BY CONTRACT SECTION:** Sections 0.1, 5.1, 8.1a, 8.2, 8.3 and 8.4;
  `shvvp_v0_6_route_gates.py`, especially `_manifest_canonical`,
  `_check_binding`, `_enforce_completeness` and `route_claims`;
  `test_shvvp_v0_6_rules.py`, especially helper `bound`, TP51b and TP51e.
- **SUBSTANCE:** The purported pre-results commitment for `SEALED` and `REALIZED`
  evidence universes is caller-asserted, not verified. `_check_binding` accepts any
  non-empty `sealed_universe_hash` or `run_log_hash` and takes
  `sealed_universe_n` or `realized_total` directly from the submitted evidence.
  Neither value is compared with a trusted commitment receipt, signed manifest,
  frozen registry entry, or other independently supplied authority. The canonical
  manifest digest binds only the component status, kind, universe identifier and the
  numerically fixed cardinalities; it intentionally contains `None` for sealed and
  realized cardinalities and contains no external hash values. Consequently, the
  caller can self-seal a favourable subset after results are known. The shipped
  tests reproduce only a `20-of-25` record in which the caller voluntarily admits
  that the sealed universe is 25. They do not attack the decisive case in which the
  caller declares the favourable 20 to be the full sealed universe and supplies a
  fresh arbitrary string as its seal. The helper `bound` in fact defaults the sealed
  or realized cardinality to the submitted denominator and fabricates a descriptive
  hash string, thereby assuming the property under test. This preserves a post-hoc
  denominator-selection escape hatch and defeats the mandatory F-P5-1 closure.
- **DISPOSITION:** Freeze a new successor version. Move the authoritative sealed and
  realized universe commitments outside the untrusted evidence record. Before
  outcome inspection, produce a reproducible commitment receipt for each applicable
  component containing at least the component key, universe identifier, expected
  cardinality, cryptographic digest, commitment time or sequence, and authority.
  The normative evaluator must receive that receipt through a trusted, separately
  authenticated input and compare the record's claimed universe and hash against
  the expected values. A non-empty or well-formed hash is insufficient; equality to
  the precommitted value must be enforced. For realized universes, bind the final
  realized total to an authoritative append-only run-log receipt fixed before
  outcome adjudication. Add regressions in which the caller self-selects a smaller
  favourable universe, supplies multiple arbitrary hashes including a one-character
  string, and attempts to replace the authoritative cardinality and run-log total.
  Each must be rejected before any share is computed.
- **CROSS-REFERENCE:** Round-5 finding F-P5-1, required disposition items 1 through
  4 and carry-forward requirement; amendment A-6.1; Section 8.1a's statement that
  cardinality is fixed by an external seal or precommitted run log; TP51b and TP51e.

## 5. Four-axis assessment

### 5.1 Falsifier integrity

**FAILED.** The fixed R4-C denominator and omission-to-`OUT OF SCOPE` attacks are
closed, but the sealed and realized universe attacks remain open because the
purported commitments are supplied by the caller and never authenticated. F-P6-1
is dispositive.

### 5.2 Empirical-anchor integrity

**PASSED.** Independently checked against the official primary records:

- Anthropic's Mythos Preview report attributes the FreeBSD NFS exploit to a
  17-year-old vulnerability, describes the 20-gadget multi-packet ROP chain, and in
  the detailed account describes a chain over 1,000 bytes split across six RPC
  requests. The protocol preserves both attributed descriptions and uses the case
  only as a motivating profile, not as a novelty label.
- The FreeBSD advisory contains the same authentication tension the protocol records:
  its problem description says the overflow does not require prior authentication,
  while its kernel-impact description refers to an authenticated user able to send
  packets to the NFS server with `kgssapi.ko` loaded. Treating the status as
  `UNKNOWN-CONTESTED` is faithful.
- AISI reports a 32-step range, 3 of 10 complete attempts, an average of 22 steps for
  Mythos Preview versus 16 for the next-best model, runs up to a 100M-token budget,
  and the absence of active defenders and defensive tooling. The protocol carries
  the caveat and does not use the result as evidence of BSSG effect.

Primary records checked:

- [Anthropic, Assessing Claude Mythos Preview's cybersecurity capabilities](https://www.anthropic.com/research/mythos-preview)
- [FreeBSD-SA-26:08.rpcsec_gss](https://www.freebsd.org/security/advisories/FreeBSD-SA-26:08.rpcsec_gss.asc)
- [UK AI Security Institute, Our evaluation of Claude Mythos Preview's cyber capabilities](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/our-evaluation-of-claude-mythos-previews-cyber-capabilities)

### 5.3 Internal consistency

**FAILED.** The normative source says external seals and run logs bind the evidence
universe, while the normative code merely accepts caller-supplied strings and
cardinalities. The source also contradicts itself about whether round 5 is pending or
divergent and whether the current contract is v0.5 or v0.6. The withdrawal of
route-level inference remains complete, and the Section 8 label set is closed at
`SUPPORTED-DESCRIPTIVE`.

### 5.4 Scope honesty

**FAILED in the executable route contract; otherwise passed in the prose.** The
protocol still prohibits the aggregate statement that SHV is validated, states the
route limits in the same sentence in Table 8.2b, disclaims significance and
population generalization, and reports no empirical result. But a favourable subset
can still be post-hoc declared to be the sealed or realized universe, allowing the
executable label to overstate what the full committed evidence would support.

## 6. Safety-boundary determination

**PASSED.** Section 7.6 still limits Phase C to offline containers, synthetic
vulnerabilities, non-deployable proofs and controlled access. It declares the
boundary non-amendable, declares weakening amendments void, and contains no later
override or hidden live-target authorization.

## 7. Withdrawn-inference determination

**PASSED.** The route path contains no normative p-value, null hypothesis,
confidence bound or false-discovery-rate procedure; all eleven route components are
Class G descriptive gates; and no route label can exceed
`SUPPORTED-DESCRIPTIVE`. F-P6-1 concerns evidence-universe authenticity, not a
reintroduction of statistical inference.

## 8. Limits of this audit

This audit does not certify future protocol execution, empirical validity, universal
correctness, external validity, or safety beyond the frozen contract. No empirical
results exist in the packet. The audit did verify byte custody, execute the supplied
checks, construct independent adversarial calls against the normative gate, inspect
the relevant source-code paths, render the complete flagship, inspect the repaired
operational tables, and recheck the two required external capability anchors against
their official primary records.

## 9. Final carrying statement

**Verdict: HALT.**

**I do not carry SHVVP v0.6 for deposit or convergence.**

The next eligible artifact is a newly frozen successor that authenticates sealed and
realized universe commitments outside caller-controlled evidence, closes the stale
current-version contradictions, updates all hashes and manifests, and undergoes a
fresh two-family carrying audit.
