# SHVVP v0.5 Audit Ledger

**What this ledger is.** The append-only record of checks executed against the v0.3 artifact set, including checks that failed during the build and were fixed, and checks that could not be executed. The protocol requires negative and null results to be recorded rather than reinterpreted, and that rule applies to the protocol's own build.

**What this ledger is NOT.** It is not a carrying audit. Every entry below was executed by the producer and editor of the artifact, whose standing is **ADVISORY and NON-CARRYING**. A green ledger is a build gate, not a certification. **No carrying PASS is claimed for v0.5.** Versions 0.1, 0.2, 0.3 and 0.4 are HALTED and were never deposited or binding.
