## Comprehensive Analysis

The five mandated commands reproduce on Round-2 bytes exactly as sealed. Every seat that executed Python (Fable 5, Gemini 3.1 Pro, plus the orchestrator on GPT Sol's behalf) confirmed 20/20 SHA, 345 tests OK, 175/175 GREEN counterexamples including CE175 F-GS-1 closure, model checker PASS across 243 kernel states + 729 antitone contracts + 11110 schedules, and the verifier's 12 PASS / 2 WARN / 0 FAIL with the DRAFT / NOT CARRIED banner intact. On the mechanical replication axis, Round-2 bytes are indistinguishable from Round 1's (Grok 4 CLEAN) and from the v2 council's re-run. That convergence is where the panel's high-confidence findings live.

The panel diverges sharply on whether the shipped closure of F-GS-1 is complete. Claude Opus 5's v2 advisory-HIGH claim A-05 — that the post-mint ceiling-write arm at `bssg_gate.py:1830-1835` fails FAIL-OPEN (absent ceiling), silently drops its `E-CEILING-RECORD-FAILED` reason code because the ALLOW receipt template at L1863 hardcodes `reason_codes: []`, and lets a post-lift HEALTHY DELETE commit end-to-end — is REPRODUCED by three independently-designed probes on Round 2. The orchestrator's `a05_probe.py` counts `ceiling_for_health` invocations and raises on the second; Fable 5's `fable5_a05_probe.py` replaces `state.contract_ceiling` with a `dict` subclass that raises on the target session's first `__setitem__`; GPT Sol's `gpt_a05_probe.py` uses a similar dict subclass with different rejection semantics. All three produce the identical trace: ALLOW receipt issued, live token, `contract_ceiling[sess]` absent, `receipt_reason_codes: []`, post-lift DELETE ALLOW, commit COMMITTED_PENDING_OUTCOME with revalidation PASS. On the mechanics, A-05 is not disputed.

The split is on severity. Fable 5 (producer family, obligated to under-attribute) argues the specific tested contract has HEALTHY ceiling `{READ, WRITE, DELETE}`, so the absent-vs-full ceiling ends up at the same landing state after the lift, meaning A-05 for this contract shape widens no authority the clean path would not also grant. Gemini 3.1 Pro argues the invariant is broken by mechanical reachability alone: a session that never demonstrably held DELETE cannot be permitted to commit it, regardless of what the contract's HEALTHY ceiling includes. GPT Sol did not weigh in on severity because its seat corrupted before writing a verdict — the same failure pattern the seat exhibited in the v2 round.

Gemini's most consequential contribution is a NEW 34th-instance defect that survives Fable 5's contract-shape nuance. In Gemini's `F-DG-1`, the first successful authorization on a fresh session is DEGRADED. The post-mint ceiling write is guarded by `health == "HEALTHY"` at L1830, so `contract_ceiling[sess]` is never written. The session's LEGITIMATE demonstrated authority is only READ. A subsequent HEALTHY DELETE attempt correctly terminalizes the session on `E-AUTHORITY-WIDENING`; an amendment then lifts it; and a post-lift HEALTHY DELETE re-establishes at the full HEALTHY ceiling because `_apply_narrowing` reads `contract_ceiling.get(session)` and sees None, taking the `_held is None` free-establishment branch. The orchestrator verified this by writing and executing `degraded_first_probe.py` on unmodified Round-2 bytes — no monkeypatch, no forced exception, native protocol. Output: `VERDICT: DEGRADED-FIRST BYPASS REPRODUCED`, `commit_outcome: COMMITTED_PENDING_OUTCOME`, `revalidation result: PASS`. Here the session's clean-path ceiling is `{READ}` (its actual demonstrated authority) and the post-lift landing state is a full `{READ, WRITE, DELETE}` grant, so the contract-shape argument Fable 5 raised for A-05 does not apply. This is a genuine widening of landing-state authority beyond what the session ever legitimately held, reached without any exception path at all.

The panel's verdict is therefore split but decisive. Fable 5's advisory (no vote weight): ADVISORY-ISSUES-FOUND, recommends A-05 be recorded as HIGH on the audit-surface reason-code drop and polarity asymmetry, and defers CRITICAL calibration to non-producer seats. Gemini 3.1 Pro (vote-weighted Tier 2): HALT — F-DG-1 is a CRITICAL native-reachability widening bypass, A-06 breaks pass-34 audit continuity, and Spec §22.2 vs L1830 is a shipped-vs-declared mismatch. GPT Sol (partial seat): reproduced A-05 mechanically via an independent probe but did not deliver an in-voice verdict; the seat's contribution reinforces the A-05 reproducibility count from two to three, but its adjudication weight for Round 2 must be treated as reduced under `bayyinah_cross-vendor-rotation.md` for Tier 1 exempted behavior.
