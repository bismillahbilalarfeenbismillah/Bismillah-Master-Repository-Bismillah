## Comprehensive Analysis

**High-confidence findings and why convergence is reliable on the audit axis.** All four seats — three non-producer load-bearing and one producer-family advisory — independently verified the three named defect closures. The F-GS-1 closure is the most consequential. Claude Opus 5's line-by-line trace of `bssg_gate.py:1808–1836` establishes that the broadening write to `contract_ceiling` runs only after `new_token()` at line 1815 has succeeded, the token has been validated for shape and bounds, and the collision check against `self.state.tokens` has passed. Every failure path exits through `_issuance_failure` (`:897–916`), which for a first-establish mint fault writes `Authority([], [])` — an empty, maximally-constrained held-authority floor — so the `_held` guard at `:1935` fires on any post-lift re-establishment. GLM 5.2 states this as a formal invariant: *`contract_ceiling` is broadened ONLY after successful token creation, validation, and collision-check.* GPT 5.6 Sol re-ran the exact pass-34 F-GS-1 probe on shipped bytes and confirmed r1's `contract_ceiling.effects = []` and r4's post-lift DELETE returning `DENY_FALSE, commit: null`; Sol additionally probed the mint-invalid arm and observed identical closure. The 10-op alphabet expansion in `bssg_model_check.py` with genuinely broader C2W target scopes is arithmetically consistent (Σ 10^i for i=1..4 = 11,110 exactly), verified by all four seats. F-GS-3's spec §26 rewording removes the surface contradiction between the spec and the shipped tool. The V32–V34 records are now present in the hash-verified `REPAIR_STATUS.md`. On the narrow question the brief asked — *has closure of F-GS-1/2/3 changed the answer?* — the code is repaired and the honest technical-soundness assessment is that the specified defects are gone.

**Areas of divergence.** The council agrees unanimously on the defect closures and unanimously on the convergence ruling, so the divergence is not in the answer but in the *newly-surfaced residuals* each seat found. Only Claude Opus 5, sitting in the producer-family advisory role with the deepest familiarity with the codebase, surfaced two same-class residuals inside the pass-35 packet: A-05 (the `except Exception:` handler at `bssg_gate.py:1830–1836` leaves the session in free-establishment state through the ceiling-**record** arm — a 34th-instance-shaped hole sitting inside the 33rd instance's fix) and A-06 (the pass-34 section of `REPAIR_STATUS.md` contains two adjacent statements that cannot both have been true at pass-34 seal time — 173/173 CE GREEN on a 7-op alphabet, and "claims 175 counterexamples"). These are advisory-only findings because a producer-family seat cannot assign HIGH severity to defects in its producer's artifact under `bayyinah_cross-vendor-rotation.md`; a non-producer seat must reproduce and re-rate. Gemini 3.1 Pro, GPT 5.6 Sol, and GLM 5.2 did not probe those specific arms — Sol and GLM concentrated on adversarial variants of the F-GS-1 vector class, and Gemini's long-context meta-audit found architectural consistency but not point-level defects. **How to weigh this:** Opus 5's A-05 is the more consequential of the two. It states a structural asymmetry — `_issuance_failure` was deliberately hardened to write empty Authority ("so the `_held` check fires"), but the ALLOW path's own `except` handler on the same class of failure produces the *unconstrained* `None` state, not empty Authority — and it names the specific remedy (write `Authority([], [])` in the `except` branch, or route to `_issuance_failure`). This is the invariant-vs-precondition tension in Opus 5's dissent made concrete. The right disposition is a fresh non-producer round on pass-35 bytes with A-05 and A-06 as first-order probes; if either reproduces, F-GS-1's closure claim requires reopening.

**Unique insights worth noting.** GLM 5.2's confirmation-bias self-critique deserves emphasis independent of the vote. GLM was one of the three original audit seats (V32–V34) that returned three consecutive PASS on the artifact that shipped with F-GS-1 latent. Its pass-35 report explicitly names this as a shared-blind-spot artifact — the exact "fixed panel" failure mode SHV Method v1.1 D6 warns against — and refuses to escalate to YES precisely because its own priors bias toward the artifact being sound. This is the discipline the methodology demands and, arguably, the strongest evidence the pass-35 review is honest: a seat with a strong prior for YES holds NO. Claude Opus 5's recurrence-vs-invariant dissent goes further, framing the V30 → V31 → V34 → pass-35 sequence as a corpus that has patched three different preconditions on the same one-line `contract_ceiling` write without ever stating the invariant. A-05 is presented as the 34th instance-shaped hole sitting inside the 33rd instance's fix — a specific structural argument for why a fresh non-producer round is not merely procedural rubber-stamping but epistemically necessary. GPT 5.6 Sol's most useful contribution beyond re-verifying its own pass-34 probe is the explicit statement that the acceptance boundary for pass-35 is exactly the shipped banner: *self-consistent repaired draft is the honest ceiling, not converged, carried, frozen, publication-ready, or shippable*.

**Final recommendation.** The council's ruling is **NO / HALT CONVERGENCE** on four independent grounds, each sufficient alone. First, PMD v1.2 §0.4 requires two consecutive cross-vendor rounds at zero CRITICAL; pass-35 has zero cross-vendor rounds on the repaired bytes, and V34 shipped with F-GS-1 CRITICAL latent, so V32–V34 cannot serve as the qualifying clean sequence. Second, SHV Method v1.1 D6 and `bayyinah_cross-vendor-rotation.md` bar producer-authored repair from self-certification; the mechanical-traceability exception fails the binary "any content beyond the remediation checklist" test because pass-35 added the C2W contract, expanded the alphabet from 7 to 10 ops, reordered ALLOW-path mutation, added CE175, and edited spec §26 and the README. The corpus applies exactly this rule to itself in `SHUDP v0.5`'s author-and-authority note: "the v0.4 convergence does not extend to the v0.5 additions." Third, SHVVP v0.8 §0.3 and §8.0 keep the two-family carry as a separate load-bearing gate that pass-35 does not touch, and under SHUDP v0.5 Axiom 5's no-default rule a carry that failed due to platform limitations yields no carry credit regardless of reason. Fourth, PMD §0.4's HIGH-residual clause fails independently: `BSSG_v0_1_DECISION_REGISTER.md` §6 lists four STILL BLOCKING items (GP-R4/GP-R8 no transition-system model, CL-B4 residue no second independent non-producer implementation, OD-11, OD-2), none carrying a project-lead-signed exception ledger entry, and the register text explicitly states that "freeze or deposit while these remain open would misrepresent the council's disposition." **Acceptance boundary:** pass-35 is a competent, honest repair whose own banner ("DRAFT / NOT CARRIED", "SELF-CONSISTENT DRAFT — not carried, not publication ready") states its correct status. **To move to QUALIFIED-YES requires the conjunction of:** (i) a fresh non-producer audit round on pass-35-or-later bytes at zero CRITICAL, including probes of Opus 5's A-05 ceiling-record-failed asymmetry and adjudication of A-06 ledger-integrity concern; followed by a second such round with at least one fresh-rotated non-producer seat per D6; (ii) SHVVP §0.3 two-family carry discharged by two non-producer families that actually execute the gate, or a project-lead-signed exception; (iii) the four STILL BLOCKING items closed or each carrying a signed exception ledger entry. Items (i) and (ii) are structurally unreachable by further producer work, which is the correct and intended consequence of SHV D6. **Vote unchanged from pass-34: NO / HALT.** Pass-35's repairs are genuine but do not, by themselves, re-establish cross-vendor convergence or discharge the carry.
