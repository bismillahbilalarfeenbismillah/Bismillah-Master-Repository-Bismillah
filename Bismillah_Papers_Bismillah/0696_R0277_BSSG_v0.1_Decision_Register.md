## 6. STILL BLOCKING

These findings are not discharged by v0.1 and the packet remains DRAFT because of them.

1. **GP-R4 and GP-R8 (from a HALT verdict).** No transition-system model and no model-checked invariants exist. Antitonicity is tested, not proved.
2. **CL-B4 residue (from a HALT verdict).** A runnable kernel now exists, but there is no second independent implementation by a non-producer lineage agreeing on the frozen vectors, so the conformance suite's adequacy (falsifier AF-11) is untested.
3. **OD-11 (Gemini, blocking-adjacent).** The specification's own diagnostic reason codes are a candidate regime signal, which is in direct tension with PR-6 clause (b). Unresolved.
4. **OD-2 (Claude C-4).** No cross-session monotonicity root is named, so rollback resistance is intra-process only.

A freeze or deposit of BSSG while these remain open would misrepresent the council's disposition.
