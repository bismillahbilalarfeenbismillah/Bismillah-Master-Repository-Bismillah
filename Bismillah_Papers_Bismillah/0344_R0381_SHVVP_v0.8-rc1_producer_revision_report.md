# SHVVP v0.8-rc1 producer revision report

**Prepared:** 2026-07-24
**Status:** producer revision candidate; not frozen, deposited, binding, or carried

## Revision basis

This candidate absorbs the complete v0.7 Seat-A HALT verdict. The two HIGH/BLOCKING findings are treated as architecture defects rather than documentation-only defects:

- **F-P7-1:** a caller could select a verification key that differed from the key represented in the frozen manifest.
- **F-P7-2:** the REALIZED-universe path authenticated a finished list but did not prove that the universe was committed before outcomes or that its event history was append-only.

The two MEDIUM findings are also absorbed:

- **F-P7-3:** `commitment_id` now resolves the exact signed Channel-A object and is verified throughout the chain.
- **F-P7-4:** the source now states the same denominator authority rule that the executable enforces.

## Implemented closure

The v0.8 route gate removes every runtime trust-root parameter. Its canonical manifest binds three distinct Ed25519 public keys: commitment authority, independent pre-run anchor, and run logger. Changing any of them changes the manifest digest and therefore the release identity.

A REALIZED universe is accepted only after verification of four linked layers: a commitment-authority-signed pre-run object, an independently signed anchor receipt over that object's digest, individually logger-signed hash-chained append events, and a commitment-authority-signed close receipt bound to the verified pre-run digest and final chain state.

The raw evidence channel may identify a commitment, but it cannot define, replace, or override the authoritative universe, denominator, authority key, append history, or closure receipt.
