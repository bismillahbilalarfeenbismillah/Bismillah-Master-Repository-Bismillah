# SHVVP v0.8-rc1 revision candidate

Structural Honesty Validation Protocol (SHVVP), v0.8-rc1, producer revision-candidate date 2026-07-24.

**Status: NOT FROZEN, NOT DEPOSITED, NOT BINDING, NOT CARRIED.** This packet is a complete audit-absorbing revision based on the v0.7 Seat-A HALT. It is intentionally an RC rather than a formal freeze because the inherited v0.7 packet declares a freeze date of 2026-07-26, while its audit and this revision were executed on 2026-07-24. That chronology must be resolved truthfully before a formal v0.8 dispatch.

## What this revision changes

- **F-P7-1 (HIGH, BLOCKING): fixed trust roots.** `route_claims` no longer accepts `trusted_pubkey` or any equivalent override. The canonical manifest binds the exact commitment-authority, independent pre-run-anchor, and run-logger public keys. Replacing a key changes `MANIFEST_SHA256` and requires a new release identity.
- **F-P7-2 (HIGH, BLOCKING): real pre-run and append-history proof.** A REALIZED universe now requires a commitment-authority-signed pre-run commitment, an independently signed anchor over its digest, individually signed hash-chained append events from a separately frozen logger, and a close receipt bound to the verified pre-run digest and final chain.
- **F-P7-3 (MEDIUM): exact commitment identifiers.** Channel-A objects are indexed by the raw record's exact signed `commitment_id`; unknown IDs, aliases, cross-component IDs, and mismatches reject before share computation.
- **F-P7-4 (MEDIUM): source/code denominator parity.** Table 8.2a now states that SEALED and REALIZED cardinalities come exclusively from authenticated Channel A, never from the raw record.
