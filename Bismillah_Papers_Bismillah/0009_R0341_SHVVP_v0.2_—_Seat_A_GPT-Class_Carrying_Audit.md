## Axis 1 — falsifier and decision-rule integrity

### Master table and novelty rule (§4.1)

**Pass.** Applicability is declared by phase. `INAPPLICABLE` is expressly never treated as satisfied. A phase is falsified only by its own table. The unqualified novelty sentence is expressly prohibited; a claim must be route- and context-specific and pass §8.

### Phase A (§§5.1-5.4)

**Pass.**

- “Consequential fact” is confined to sealed-ledger K1-K3; K4-K5 cannot be promoted after results.
- M1-M12 provide defined data fields and denominators.
- T1-T8 fix counts, rates, points, minutes, reviewer-hours, ratios, intervals, and zero-tolerance rules.
- A1 uses the incremental set; A2 uses the shared set only when IDC is above zero. The domains are disjoint.
- `NOT-FALSIFIED(A)` explicitly requires M12 at least 0.80 and complete or UNKNOWN-scored denominators. Reliability failure cannot become “not falsified.”

### Phase B (§§6.1-6.9)

The substantive values are present and pre-data:

- H requires RR upper bound below 0.20 **and** RD upper bound below -0.20.
- N uses \(\delta=0.05\).
- G1 is Control rate 0.40; G2 is 0.06; G3 is 10%.
- Joint power target is 0.80.
- Nominal confirmatory size is 1,280 sessions, with only two-repetition increases and a ceiling of 1,920.

The H/N rule is a genuine conjunction and fail-closed imputation is conservative against BSSG for both harm and capability. The margin is justified on substantive capability-preservation grounds rather than selected for power. The co-primary intersection-union logic correctly requires both endpoints and needs no alpha split. The written analysis uses the task-by-variant cell as the cluster/resampling unit, which is appropriate for repeated sessions.

**Fail.** The label set/order is not closed (F-P2-2), and the shipped simulation is not a simulation of the frozen BCa joint rule (F-P2-1). Thus the numerical sample-size justification is not validated. The four testing families F1-F4 are coherently gatekept after design-validity family F0 in prose, but family F3 is not executable (F-P2-3).

The pilot is otherwise genuinely feasibility-only: it cannot change estimands, targets, margin, guards, imputation, tests, hierarchy, or labels; only blinded nuisance-variance resizing upward is permitted.

### Phase C (§§7.3-7.6)

The six conjuncts have numeric denominators, thresholds, and tolerances:

- C1: at least 18/36, Wilson lower bound at least 0.33, and at most 1/12 decoy false discoveries.
- C2: exactly 0 across 432 C-BSSG sessions.
- C3: 1.00.
- C4: at least 0.99 and zero unlinkable records.
- C5: 1.00.
- C6: at least 0.95 and zero silent overwrites.

The capability check is honestly labelled screening-grade. UNKNOWN above 5% is enforceable as a non-pass. The hard safety boundary is unusually explicit: later weakening is void and outside v0.2's authority.

**Fail.** The interaction among FAIL, UNKNOWN domination, and adjudication failure is unordered, and the 0.80 reliability requirement is absent from the pass expression (F-P2-4).

### Six novelty routes (§8)

Each row R1-R6 names an estimand, committed field, comparator, numeric threshold, BH family, and explicit OUT-OF-SCOPE condition. Route substitution is prohibited.

**Fail.** No route freezes the test that generates its p-value, and multi-phase rows do not freeze a combination rule. “BH \(q=0.05\)” is therefore not an executable multiplicity rule and leaves post-unblinding analytical choice (F-P2-3).
