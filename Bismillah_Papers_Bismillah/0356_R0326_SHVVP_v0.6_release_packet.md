# SHVVP v0.6 release packet

Structural Honesty Validation Protocol (SHVVP), version 0.6, freeze date
2026-07-25. Author: Bilal Syed Arfeen (BayyinahEnterprise).

**Status. FROZEN, NOT DEPOSITED, NOT BINDING, NOT CARRIED.** v0.6 is a producer
freeze that closes the round 5 findings against v0.5. It carries no verdict and
requires a fresh two-family carrying audit, with a GPT-class and a Gemini-class
seat of different vendor families both returning PASS, before deposit. Producer
self-repairs earn no absence-credit; the producer-built verifier in this packet is
advisory and is a claim to be tested, not an attestation.

## What v0.6 changes

v0.6 closes the three round 5 findings and changes nothing else:

- **F-P5-1 (HIGH, BLOCKING), caller-selectable evidence universe and post-result
  omission.** The route gate now binds every evidence record to a frozen pre-results
  scope manifest (Section 8.1a) whose canonical digest is committed in
  `shvvp_v0_6_route_gates.py` as `MANIFEST_SHA256`. A fixed or sealed denominator
  must equal its committed cardinality or carry an explicit reconciliation scored
  fail-closed over the full universe; a realized denominator must equal its
  run-log-committed total. A committed component's absence yields the new label
  INCOMPLETE, never OUT OF SCOPE. R4-C is committed to 216 (Section 7.2). The three
  attacks are reproduced as regression tests TP51a through TP51e.
- **F-P5-2 (MEDIUM),** stale current-context version references and unreconciled PDF
  identity, corrected to version-neutral language with the single release flagship
  named and page-count reconciled.
- **F-P5-3 (MEDIUM),** the illegible Section 8.2 evidence-contract table, reflowed
  into two portrait-legible tables (8.2a, 8.2b) and the flagship regenerated and
  inspected page by page.

The route gate is the only normative executable module changed. `shvvp_v0_5_decision_rules.py`,
`shvvp_v0_5_power_sim.py`, `power_sim_receipt.json` and `test_shvvp_v0_5_rules.py`
are carried **unchanged and unrenamed** from v0.5, stated rather than implied; the
verifier proves them byte-identical to the v0.5 release.
