ANALYTICAL LENS: DIFFERENTIATOR FRAMEWORK (Seat S01)
=====================================================

The Differentiator Framework applies ten analytical layers to every
claim. Each layer is a filter; a defect that survives all ten layers
is deeply embedded. Your task is to apply these layers to the v0.55
entry's prose claims and report which layers catch what.

THE TEN LAYERS
---------------

Layer 1 (Time-Geometry): Place each claim on a timeline. When was the
  claim first stated? Has it been updated at every version advance?
  The R13 Class A defects (title, status, FB-7b) were time-geometry
  failures: claims frozen at a prior version's timestamp.

Layer 2 (Elliott Wave): Does the defect count exhibit a wave structure?
  Is R14 at a wave peak (correction overshoot), trough (exhaustion),
  or inflection (class transition)?

Layer 3 (Fractal Self-Similarity): Does the same defect class appear
  at multiple recursion levels? If the register's cross-reference error
  is the same class as the body's version-token error, the generator
  is at Level 0.

Layer 4 (Momentum): Is the defect count per round accelerating,
  decelerating, or stable? R7: many. R12: 1. R13: 3 (body-level).
  What does the momentum predict for R14?

Layer 5 (Awareness Variable): Split claims into high-awareness
  (the producer knew to check) and low-awareness (the producer did
  not know to check). Which category produces more Class A findings?

Layer 6 (Incompleteness): What CAN'T this audit see? What are the
  structural blind spots of your seat? State them explicitly.

Layer 7 (GAN Dynamics): Generator (producer) vs. discriminator (you).
  Is the producer's output improving faster than your ability to find
  defects? Or are you finding the same class every round?

Layer 8 (Cobweb/Oscillation): Does the defect alternate between loci?
  R12 was in Section 27 (entry count). R13 was in Sections 0/25
  (body). Does R14 oscillate back to Section 27?

Layer 9 (Adversarial Probing Score): Rate each claim on a 0-1 scale
  for vulnerability to adversarial mutation. Which claims are most
  fragile?

Layer 10 (Diagnostic-Adversarial): Hold both readings simultaneously.
  The register says "3-seat convergence" -- under what interpretation
  could this be correct? Under what interpretation is it wrong? Which
  interpretation is supported by bytes?

APPLICATION TO R14
-------------------

Apply all ten layers to the v0.55 entry. For each finding, state
which layers caught it and which layers missed it. A finding caught
by 8+ layers is robust. A finding caught by 1-2 layers may be a
false positive. Report the layer distribution for each finding.

The Differentiator lens is the widest-angle lens. It catches defects
the other lenses miss because it applies ten orthogonal filters. The
trade-off is depth: each layer gets less analytical time than the
single-lens seats. Compensate by focusing the deepest analysis on
the highest-probability targets from PREDICTIVE_ANALYSIS.txt.
