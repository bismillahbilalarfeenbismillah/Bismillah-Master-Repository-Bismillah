## Filed findings

### F-R8-PUB-1 - carrying-evidence verifier and proof-language closure incomplete

**Severity:** HIGH, BLOCKING.

The new evidence directory is a material improvement, and the relevant cryptographic outputs reproduce. The closure is nevertheless incomplete for two independent reasons:

1. `verify_seat_evidence.py` says it confirms six Kimi forgery strings but its `EXPECT` mapping contains only `noanchor`, `atkanchor`, `wrongdig`, and `predate`. It omits the attacker-signed sealed-subset and post-hoc realized-bundle strings. It also hardcodes values rather than parsing the raw seat reports, so it cannot detect divergence between the reports and its own constants.
2. The carrying record first states the correct limitation—challenge hashes do not by themselves establish model identity, independence, or execution of the gate—but later says: `the matched hash IS the proof.` This is internally contradictory and fails the explicit re-audit condition.

**Required correction:** make the verifier parse the two report files, reproduce and compare all six Kimi exception strings plus both challenge hashes, fail on missing/extra/mismatched fields, and replace the residual “IS the proof” sentence with the already-stated limited claim.

### F-R8-PUB-10 - pre-deposit status parity remains incomplete

**Severity:** LOW, but blocking under the dispatched all-defects-closed rule.

The leading source, README, carrying record, and PDF title surface correctly say deposit-ready/prepared for deposit. Three current paths still assert completed deposit:

- `verify_shvvp_v0_8.py` optional receipt: `FROZEN, CARRIED, DEPOSITED`;
- current source/PDF Section 8.1b: `DEPOSITED preregistration instrument`;
- carrying record: `deposited here` and `deposited bytes`.

**Required correction:** use `FROZEN, CARRIED, DEPOSIT-READY` or equivalent on every current status surface; reserve “deposited” for a post-deposit record/version.
