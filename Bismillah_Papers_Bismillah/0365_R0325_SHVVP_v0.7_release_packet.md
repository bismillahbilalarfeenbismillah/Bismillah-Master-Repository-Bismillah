# SHVVP v0.7 release packet

Structural Honesty Validation Protocol (SHVVP), version 0.7, freeze date
2026-07-26. Author: Bilal Syed Arfeen (BayyinahEnterprise).

**Status. FROZEN, NOT DEPOSITED, NOT BINDING, NOT CARRIED.** v0.7 is a producer
freeze that closes the round 6 findings against v0.6. It carries no verdict and
requires a fresh two-family carrying audit, with a GPT-class and a Gemini-class seat
of different vendor families both returning PASS after **executing** the gate,
before deposit. Producer self-repairs earn no absence-credit; the producer-built
verifier here is advisory and is a claim to be tested.

## What v0.7 changes

- **F-P6-1 (HIGH, BLOCKING), caller-asserted sealed/realized universe commitments.**
  v0.6 closed the FIXED-denominator subset attack but read every SEALED and REALIZED
  cardinality and hash from the untrusted evidence record, checking only that they
  were non-empty, so a favorable subset could be self-declared to be the whole
  universe. v0.7 makes it a trust-boundary correction (Section 8.1b): the raw record
  (Channel B) may only reference a commitment, and any cardinality, hash, total or
  signature in it is rejected; the authoritative universe is supplied by the operator
  through Channel A, a custodian-signed sealed commitment or a signed run-close
  receipt over an append-only hash-chained log, verified against the committing
  authority whose public key is frozen into `MANIFEST_SHA256`. The gate obtains a
  cardinality only from an authenticated commitment and enforces equality; a
  well-formed or non-empty hash is never sufficient.
- **F-P6-2 (MEDIUM),** residual stale current-context references, corrected, with the
  verifier's five-phrase scanner replaced by a structured current-status parity
  check.

The route gate is the only normative executable module changed. `shvvp_v0_5_decision_rules.py`,
`shvvp_v0_5_power_sim.py`, `power_sim_receipt.json` and `test_shvvp_v0_5_rules.py`
are carried **unchanged and unrenamed** from v0.5; the verifier proves them
byte-identical.

    python3 test_shvvp_v0_7_rules.py
    python3 verify_shvvp_v0_7.py --v05-release <extracted v0.5 release dir>

Both are advisory. The verifier re-runs the F-P6-1 self-sealing attack inline with an
ephemeral key, so it does not depend on the test file's fixtures. Treat every PASS as
a claim to test against the canonical source and the code.

## The commitment channel, for the auditor

`route_claims(raw_evidence, commitments, run_logs, trusted_pubkey)` is called by the
OPERATOR. To reproduce a legitimate evaluation you generate a custodian keypair, sign
sealed commitments and run-close receipts with `sealed_commitment_payload` and
`run_close_payload`, and pass the public key as `trusted_pubkey`. To reproduce the
attack, declare a favorable subset to be the whole universe and confirm the gate
rejects it unless the authoritative commitment says so. The evidence submitter never
controls the commitment bundle or the key.
