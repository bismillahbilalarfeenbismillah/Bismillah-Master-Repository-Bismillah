### Footer pattern: retroactive edit vs rolling doc-invariant

Observed distribution of `This document claims N counterexamples.` footers:
L33 (pass-35 section) = 176, L71 (pass-34 section) = 176, L279 (pass-30
post-V28 section) = 176; then L364 (pass-29) = 159, L433 = 156, L486 = 154,
and so on, frozen at historical values down the document.

Interpretation A (retroactive edit, Gemini's original concern): the footer is
part of each pass section's historical record, and the pass-30-post-V28 footer
reading 176 (a corpus size that did not exist at pass-30; pass-29 sealed 159
and repair33 sealed 172) is an after-the-fact rewrite of history. Under this
reading the edit is also inconsistently applied, since pass-29 and older
footers were left at their historical values.

Interpretation B (rolling doc-invariant, COUNCIL_INSTRUCTIONS reading): the
footer is a document-level assertion of the CURRENT corpus size, stamped by
the generator on the sections it still maintains, and it is metadata rather
than history. The decisive evidence for B is that the actual historical
record, the battery-state lines, is demonstrably unedited (L67 = 173/173,
L119 = 172/172 exactly as sealed in those passes), and the footer wording is
present-tense and document-scoped ("This document claims"), not pass-scoped
("this pass sealed").

Assessment: the substrate supports Interpretation B for the load-bearing
question (no historical battery evidence was altered, so provenance is
intact), but the two interpretations are observationally distinguishable only
because the battery-state lines exist as an independent unedited record. The
residual ambiguity is that under B the frozen old footers (159, 156, ...) are
now FALSE as document-level assertions, so the document mixes two footer
semantics. This is recorded as advisory finding F-FB-3 (LOW) below. It does
not affect the fix verdict: F-P36-01 is correctly applied.

mixed semantics across sections.

- **Locus:** `REPAIR_STATUS.md` footers at L33, L71, L279 (read 176, current
  corpus) vs L364, L433, L486, L523, L579, L628, L675 (frozen historical
  values 159 down to 140).
- **Substance:** The rolling doc-invariant interpretation (COUNCIL_
  INSTRUCTIONS) holds for the maintained sections and no historical
  battery-state line was edited, but the generator rolls only a subset of
  section footers forward. Older footers are now false if read under the
  rolling-invariant semantics, and newer footers are anachronistic if read
  under the historical-record semantics. A future auditor without the
  battery-state cross-check could reasonably reach Gemini's retroactive-edit
  reading.
- **Disposition:** No code change. Documentation hygiene options, either is
  sufficient: (a) roll ALL footers to the current corpus size every pass, or
  (b) add one line near the top of REPAIR_STATUS.md stating that the footer
  is a document-level current-corpus assertion stamped on maintained sections
  and that battery-state lines are the immutable historical record.
- **Cross-reference:** F-P36-01 (this round, verified fixed); pass-36
  synthesis Gemini footer concern; `bayyinah_severity-rubric.md` LOW band
  (future-reader hygiene).

**F-FB-4** LOW (advisory-only) -- `_class_fix_empty_ceiling` probe tests the
init invariant only; its claim text promises the post-lift bound.
