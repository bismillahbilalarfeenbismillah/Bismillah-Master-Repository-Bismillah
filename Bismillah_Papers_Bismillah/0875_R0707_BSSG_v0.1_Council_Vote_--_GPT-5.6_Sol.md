## 3. Convergence answer

# **NO**

Three-seat V32-V34 audit agreement is **not sufficient to accept BSSG v0.1 convergence/carry** on this record.

1. **The zero-blocker premise is now falsified.** F-GS-1 is a reproduced runtime authority-introduction path ending in a committed effect that the session never successfully held before recovery. A CRITICAL finding makes the artifact unfit to ship and requires HALT-and-escalate; PMD convergence requires two consecutive cross-vendor rounds with zero CRITICAL, zero or excepted HIGH, resolved MEDIUM/LOW, and all standing checks passing. (Bayyinah Severity Rubric, "CRITICAL" and "Severity boundary calibration"; PMD v1.2 / BAF v3.0, §0.4.)

2. **Even absent F-GS-1, audit convergence does not substitute for carry.** SHVVP requires carrying seats to be non-producer lineages of different families, makes producer review advisory, and treats divergence as dispositive against deposit. Its carried v0.8 status additionally binds carry to executing seats and byte-identical artifacts. (SHVVP v0.8, §10 "Council and adjudication discipline" and Status; SHVVP v0.8, §0.3 version/carry record.) BSSG's own canonical draft is stricter still: §29 says the council seats do not count as carry and requires a non-producer independent kernel implementation reproducing every frozen vector. The failed DeepSeek/Qwen attempt therefore remains an unfulfilled gate, regardless of whether the failure arose in artifact logic or delivery/execution capability.

3. **A capability-shortfall is not affirmative evidence.** DeepSeek's non-execution and Qwen's corrupted delivery establish neither defect nor successful exact-byte execution. Missing independent evidence must remain UNKNOWN rather than default to the favorable value. (SHUDP v0.5, Axiom 5 subsection, canonical form and no-default rule; SHVVP v0.8, §10; SHV Method v1.1, D6.) Thus the fair conclusion is not "carry failed because BSSG failed"; it is "carry remains unperformed."

4. **Three rounds do not erase an independently required fresh-rotation condition.** PMD requires two consecutive cross-vendor clean rounds, but the rotation rule also requires the auditor to differ from both producer and prior-round auditor. The supplied history names the same three vendor families across V32-V34 and does not establish fresh instances for the load-bearing rounds. More rounds exceed the numeric count but do not prove the orthogonal freshness predicate. (PMD v1.2 / BAF v3.0, §0.4; Bayyinah Cross-Vendor Rotation, "Rotation rule" and "same-instance exception"; SHV Method v1.1, D6.)

5. **Residual MODERATE documentation findings are not blockers only if properly absorbed or explicitly moved to non-operative future reference.** They do not prevent PMD convergence merely by existing, but they also do not disappear by relabeling; PMD §0.4 requires their disposition and all standing checks to pass. (PMD v1.2 / BAF v3.0, §0.3-§0.4; Bayyinah Severity Rubric, "MEDIUM".)

6. **K=5 strengthens only the same bounded claim.** It adds one schedule step over the same seven-operation alphabet and same successful C1 initialization; it does not include token-mint failure, first-establishment failure, commit, or a broader target scope. It therefore neither covers F-GS-1 nor becomes a second exact-byte implementation/carry. (SHV Method v1.1, D3-D4 and D6; SHVVP v0.8, §10; SHUDP v0.5, Axiom 5 boundary declaration/no-default discipline.)

7. **PMD M1.5 does not excuse this review.** The mechanical-traceability exception applies only when an absorption cycle exclusively follows prescribed prior dispositions with zero architectural latitude, and even then cross-instance review remains recommended and project-lead signoff is required. The authorization-ceiling repairs changed load-bearing state-transition semantics, and this review found a new route; the exception's antecedent is false. ## 5. Dissenting angle

I disagree with the producer framing that the DeepSeek/Qwen failure can be bypassed because it was caused by platform limitations rather than an artifact defect. That fact removes a negative inference against BSSG, but it does not create the positive, independent execution evidence the frozen carry rule requires. Treating "not tested because unavailable" as "satisfied by other audit seats" is precisely the favorable default prohibited by the evidence-boundary discipline. (SHUDP v0.5, Axiom 5 canonical form and no-default rule; SHVVP v0.8, §10; SHV Method v1.1, D6.)

The stronger dissent is now concrete rather than procedural: V32-V34 missed a runtime ceiling-poison path that their own repair comments identify as the critical class. Therefore repeated agreement by the same three audit families cannot be used as evidence that carry would be redundant. Multi-chain convergence is valuable, but it certifies only what independent checkers actually re-derived within declared boundaries. (SHV Method v1.1, D3-D6; PMD v1.2 / BAF v3.0, §0.4; SHVVP v0.8, §8.0.)
