3. Architecture: Seven Layers, Seven Witnesses, Twenty-Four Positions
The verification architecture has three structural dimensions:
Depth (seven layers): nested verification levels, each encompassing everything below it. A failure at any layer
blocks everything above. These are the Seven Heavens of the cosmological model -- the vertical axis of the
verification space.
Breadth (seven witnesses): distinct instantiations of the same verification. Each witness receives the same
dispatch, encounters the same artifact, produces its own findings. Their convergence proves the finding is in the


artifact, not in the witness. These are the Seven Earths -- the horizontal axis.
Resolution (twenty-four positions): specific, named verification checkpoints distributed across the seven layers.
Each position is a testable predicate. A finding lives at coordinates (witness, position). Convergence across
witnesses at the same position is the strongest evidence.
The complete verification space is 7 witnesses x 24 positions = 168 cells. Every finding maps to a coordinate.
Every convergence maps to a column.

7. Emergent Analytical Character
Each witness's model architecture produces findings with a characteristic pattern -- a natural emphasis on
certain positions, a natural insensitivity to others. This is the witness's analytical character. It emerges from what
the witness produces on the shipped bytes; it is not assigned by the dispatch, and it is not named or tabled in
advance.
When the triage observes two witnesses from different families independently reporting findings at the same
position, this is convergence -- evidence that the finding is in the artifact, not in either witness. When the triage
observes a single finding that manifests at multiple positions across different layers, this is cross-layer
resonance -- evidence that a single underlying defect exists at the fundamental level, not just at one surface.
Cross-layer resonance is stronger evidence than same-layer convergence. Same-layer convergence could
reflect a shared architectural sensitivity. Cross-layer resonance proves the defect exists independently of the
verification surface. The triage observes and reports these patterns. It does not prescribe them.

9. The Barzakh Gate
