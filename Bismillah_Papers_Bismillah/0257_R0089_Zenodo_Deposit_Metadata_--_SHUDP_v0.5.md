2026 OpenAI / Hugging Face model-evaluation escape and cross-organizational compromise. The July 22,

four-axiom canon then available, and v0.5 adds A5 only after the companion paper's publication.

event-level adjudicative verdict remains UNKNOWN. Two independent carrying council seats of different
