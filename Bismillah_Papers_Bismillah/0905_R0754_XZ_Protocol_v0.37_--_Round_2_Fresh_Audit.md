## FINDINGS

### F-GLM52-R2F-01 (HIGH, Class C — disclosed boundary): Section 22 item 7 exception is an evaluation-function modification

**Locus:** Section 22 item 7, operative clause added v0.37 (xz_protocol_v0_37.md line 700).

**Finding:** The v0.37 amendment to Section 22 item 7 creates a "persistent architectural HALT" exception category that allows FROZEN status to survive active binding HALTs from carrying seats. In GAN terms (Layer 7), this is an evaluation-function modification: the producer (generator) modified the discriminator's (carrying seats') stopping criterion rather than improving the generator's output.

The original discriminator rule (inherited from SHVVP, unchanged through v0.36): "a HALT from either carrying seat blocks freeze outright and requires a new version." The v0.37 amendment creates an exception when three conditions are met: (a) the HALTing seat has audited at least two consecutive versions, (b) each version met prior clearance conditions and the seat re-HALTed on new bases, (c) six or more carrying seats returned non-HALT.

The structural diagnosis: GLM-5.2 carry-1C and Kimi K3 carry-1A entered a non-converging loop where the disposition of each HALT generates findings in the description of the disposition, which generates a new HALT basis. This is a GAN training pathology — the discriminator's feedback loop generates its own training signal, producing an unbounded sequence of HALTs without convergence. The producer's response was to modify the discriminator's stopping criterion (creating the exception) rather than to address the root cause (the disposition-generates-finding loop).

**Tier:** 2 (structural pattern, verified from the amendment record).

**What would disprove it:** Demonstration that the two HALTing seats' re-HALT bases at v0.35 are genuinely novel defects not generated by the disposition process itself — i.e., that the HALTs are independent findings rather than artifacts of the disposition loop.

**Disposition:** DISCLOSED as standing boundary. The exception is honestly disclosed with strict applicability conditions. The amendment is a visible body change, not a silent edit. However, the exception changes the operative carry rule, and no gate checks the FROZEN status token or the item 7 operative clause (standing boundary #16). The exception's conditions (a)-(c) are semantic, not gated.
