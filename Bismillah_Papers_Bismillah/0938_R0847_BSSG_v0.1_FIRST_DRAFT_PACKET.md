## 4. What the reference gate does and does not do

Enforced by `bssg_gate.py`: recomputation of all five conjuncts from raw evidence; refusal of any caller-supplied derived authorization fact; UNKNOWN never satisfied; authority epoch monotonicity and monotone narrowing with a strictness rule; binding of the authorization to request digest, contract digest, policy digest, witness receipt digest, evidence epoch, nonce, and expiry; one-time commit tokens redeemed by compare-and-swap; commit-time revalidation of epochs and bindings; rejection of replay, in-process rollback, stale evidence, unknown and extra fields, malformed timestamps, clock ambiguity beyond the declared skew, witness-control ambiguity, mismatched digests, target and effect widening, and recovery from a terminal state without an independent amendment.

Not established by `bssg_gate.py`: complete mediation, key custody, cryptography of any kind (signature verification is modelled through an injected verifier), witness honesty, evidence independence as fact, cross-restart rollback resistance, cross-process or distributed concurrency safety, or any outcome. (The reference does provide the NARROW process-local, single-lock serialization described in Section 6; it does not establish concurrency safety in general. Kimi-P15-11.)

**Unresolved blockers. The packet remains DRAFT because of these, and they are not discharged.**

1. No transition-system model and no model-checked invariants exist. Authority antitonicity is tested, not proved. This is part of the GPT 5.6 Sol HALT.
2. No second independent implementation by a non-producer lineage agrees on the frozen vectors, so falsifier AF-11, the adequacy of the conformance suite itself, is untested. This is the residue of the Claude Opus 5 HALT.
3. OD-11 is unresolved and blocking-adjacent: the specification's own diagnostic reason codes are a candidate observation-regime signal, in direct tension with PR-6 clause (b).
4. OD-2 is unresolved: no external monotonicity root is named, so rollback resistance is intra-process only.

**Known weaknesses the producer chose not to hide.**

- Contracts and policies are not schemas at v0.1, because the charter fixes six schema names. The ceiling function on which the strict-narrowing rule depends therefore lives in a normatively undefined contract structure (OD-10).
- The evidence-age and clock-skew defaults in the reference gate, 300 seconds and 5 seconds, are placeholders with no evidentiary basis (OD-3).
- Threshold witness counting, transparency-log inclusion, revocation epochs, and control-plane provenance are specified at profile level but not implemented.
- The reference gate keeps monotonicity state in process memory and serializes every public state transition through a single lock (process-local concurrent-redemption safety). Cross-process / distributed atomicity is a deployment obligation (BSSG-H), not a property of this reference.
- The GPT 5.6 Sol objection that evidence independence cannot be optional at the Minimal profile was rejected by the producer in favour of the disclosure route; that rejection is a producer choice, not a resolution, and it is recorded as such.

**What this packet does not claim.** It does not claim council approval. It does not claim carriage. It does not claim that any property holds in any deployment. It does not claim that the architecture changes any outcome.
