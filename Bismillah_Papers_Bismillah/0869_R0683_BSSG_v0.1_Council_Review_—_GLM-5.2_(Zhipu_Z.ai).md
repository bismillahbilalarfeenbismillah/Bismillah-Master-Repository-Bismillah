### 3.3 SHV D6 — Producer-quarantined, multi-chain convergence (MET)

> "Certify a finding only through multiple independent checkers who share no lineage with the producer and no state with its creation, drawn from more than one independent source or method or model family. **Assessment:** The closure is sound across all three layers. I find no hole.

### 5.2 K=4 vs K=5 gap — does K=5 catch anything K=4 misses?

**Schedule math:**
- K=4, 7-op alphabet: \(\sum_{i=1}^{4} 7^i = 2800\) schedules. Shipped.
- K=5, 7-op alphabet: \(\sum_{i=1}^{5} 7^i = 19607\) schedules. GLM independent verification.
- K=5 adds 16807 length-5 schedules (7× expansion).

**Structural argument for redundancy:** The BOUNDED-AUTH-INTRO check tests each ALLOW independently against the granted set. If no ALLOW in any 4-op schedule violates, the question is whether a 5th operation could produce a violating ALLOW when the first 4 didn't. Since `contract_ceiling` can only narrow after establishment (the ceiling-poison fix ensures this), the session authority after 4 operations is a subset of the establishment ceiling. A 5th operation on this narrowed state cannot produce an ALLOW outside the granted set. Therefore K=4 is structurally sufficient, and K=5 is over-coverage.

**Where the structural argument could fail:**
1. If `contract_ceiling` can widen after establishment — but the code prevents this (V30+V31 fixes, verified above).
2. If the 7-op alphabet misses a vector — but the alphabet was specifically designed to cover the known vectors (C1/C2 × READ/WRITE/DELETE × HEALTHY/DEGRADED + LIFT).
3. If the antitone check (729 contracts) has a hole — but the antitone check is a separate exhaustive verification of ceiling monotonicity.

**Assessment:** K=5 is valuable as independent brute-force verification of the structural argument, but it does not catch anything K=4 misses given the soundness of the ceiling-poison closure. The 7× expansion provides strong confirmation, not new coverage.

## 6. Dissenting angle

**The SHVVP §0.3/§8.0 carry gate is not fully discharged, and the three-seat convergence does not strictly substitute for it.**

The SHVVP carry rule requires "two executing non-producer families of different vendors" as a **separate** load-bearing gate (SHVVP §0.3, §8.0). In the SHVVP v0.8 precedent, the carry families (Kimi, Qwen) were specifically designated for the carry, and the GPT family was "advisory" — the carry families were distinct from the design contributor.

In the BSSG case, the carry pool (DeepSeek, Qwen) was designated as the carry, separate from the audit seats (GPT, GLM, Kimi). The carry failed. The audit seats can serve double-duty as carry families (as Kimi did for SHVVP v0.8), but this is a **substitution**, not a strict satisfaction of the carry gate. The carry gate was designed to provide verification independent of the audit process — using the audit seats as the carry collapses this separation.

My QUALIFIED-YES acknowledges this: convergence is sufficient to ship the artifact as a DRAFT (which is what the gate banner says), but a future carry by families outside the audit seats would strengthen the evidence beyond what three-seat convergence provides. The qualification is that the carry's *separation-from-audit* requirement is not met, only its *independence-from-producer* and *execution* requirements are met.

This dissent does not change my vote. The methodology's mechanical-traceability exception (PMD §0.5 item 5 / M1.5) explicitly states that cross-instance re-audit is "recommended-but-not-blocking" for mechanical propagations, and the three-seat convergence provides three independent executing non-producer families — exceeding the carry's independence threshold even if not its separation threshold.
