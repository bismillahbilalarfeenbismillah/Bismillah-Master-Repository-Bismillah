## 3. Convergence re-ruling

I maintain my pass-34 **QUALIFIED-NO** vote, now issued as **NO** because the gating requirement is definitively unfulfilled.

**Methodological grounding:**
1.  **The Carry Gate (SHVVP §0.3 and §10):** SHVVP explicitly requires a successful carry by non-producer families to discharge the artifact. The pass-34 attempt failed due to execution limits. Pass-35 is a repair dispatch authored by the producer (Anthropic), which, under SHV Method D6, is non-certifying: *"The producer advises but does not certify itself."* A producer-authored repair cannot substitute for a clean, non-producer carry. The carry remains completely undischarged.
2.  **Zero-CRITICAL Requirement (PMD §0.4):** Convergence requires two consecutive cross-vendor rounds with 0 CRITICAL findings. Pass-34 yielded a CRITICAL finding (F-GS-1). Adding post-hoc counterexamples (CE175) does not retroactively make pass-34 a clean round. A fresh cross-vendor round is strictly required to reset the counter and achieve the two-round threshold.
3.  **Missing Evidence (SHUDP Axiom 5):** The absence of non-producer attestation constitutes missing evidence of convergence. Under SHUDP Axiom 5 (no-default rule), an unevaluated or missing property defaults to UNKNOWN, which prevents a PASS assertion. We cannot assume a successful carry.
4.  **Still Blocking Design Decisions (G10 WARN):** The artifact ships with 12 open design decisions, four of which are explicitly marked as "STILL BLOCKING" in the Decision Register (`BSSG_v0_1_DECISION_REGISTER.md`). Specifically, the lack of a transition-system model (GP-R4, GP-R8), lack of a second independent implementation (CL-B4 residue), unresolved diagnostic signaling (OD-11), and lack of a cross-session monotonicity root (OD-2). PMD §0.4 explicitly requires HIGH residuals to be 0 or accompanied by a project-lead-signed exception. These are explicitly blocking, precluding convergence.

The **mechanical-traceability exception** (PMD M1.5 / §0.5 clause 5) does not apply here to bypass the audit. ## 5. Dissenting angle

However, under SHV Method D8 ("fabricate nothing"), a test written *after* the vulnerability is exposed only proves that the patch works for that specific vector; it does not prove the absence of adjacent logic flaws that a genuinely independent, adversarial audit round might uncover. The fact that V32-V34 shared a "blind spot" on the ceiling-poison write-before-mint sequence means the methodology's reliance on continuous adversarial review is justified. Producer-authored repairs, no matter how exhaustive the new test suite, are inherently biased toward the known flaw. A completely fresh adversarial generation round is epistemologically necessary to confirm the structural integrity of the `bssg_gate.py` state machine, especially given the history of recurring logic regressions in this artifact.
