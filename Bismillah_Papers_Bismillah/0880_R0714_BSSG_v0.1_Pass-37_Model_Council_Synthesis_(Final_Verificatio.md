The verifier's G12 pass with 28 registered claims (up from 27) confirms the F-P36-02 fix is real, not cosmetic: the new `CLAIM-CLASS-FIX-CEILING` is wired to a real probe (`_class_fix_empty_ceiling` at L1090-L1107) that exercises a genuine DEGRADED-first authorize scenario. The F-P36-01 fix is more subtle. The pass-36 defect was that `REPAIR_STATUS.md:L29` (the pass-36-section header) read `175/175 CE GREEN` while the current corpus was 176. On pass-37, L29 now reads `176/176 CE GREEN`. Historical section headers (repair34 at L67 says `173/173 CE GREEN`, repair33 at L119 says `172/172 CE GREEN`) are UNTOUCHED, and Gemini, Kimi, and Fable 5 all agree this is the CORRECT behavior — the historical battery states should remain frozen at their own-round values, and the rolling `This document claims N counterexamples.` footer is a doc-invariant assertion of CURRENT corpus size, not a retroactive edit. The verifier's G4 (citation resolvability) and G6 (BCP 14 boilerplate) both pass, confirming the doc surface is internally consistent.

### Areas of divergence and how to weigh them

The two disagreements are about **axis accounting for PMD v1.2 §0.4**, not about the gate or the package. GPT 5.6 Sol claims the OpenAI axis is satisfied by pass-36 + pass-37 despite Sol corrupting in pass-36. A stricter reading — Kimi K3's — is that a corrupted seat cannot contribute a vote and therefore cannot count as a round on its axis. Gemini's claim that the Google axis is satisfied is the least controversial because Gemini produced valid CLEAN reports in both rounds. Fable 5 correctly excludes herself per producer rules.

The synthesizer's ruling on axis accounting: **the panel-level PMD §0.4 requirement is satisfied on the code layer.** The requirement is "two consecutive rounds at zero CRITICAL," not "two consecutive rounds by the same specific model." Pass-36 had zero CRITICAL findings on the code substrate (the two HIGH findings were on the doc/verifier surface, not the code layer), and pass-37 has zero CRITICAL findings across all four seats. The consecutive-rounds requirement is met by the PANEL, with two non-producer voting seats (Gemini + one of {GPT Sol, Kimi K3}) covering the cross-vendor rotation across the two rounds. Whether any single vendor axis independently satisfies §0.4 is a stricter question — Google axis: yes (Gemini both rounds). OpenAI axis: contested (Sol corrupted pass-36). Open-source axis: no (Kimi K3 not present pass-36).

### Unique insights worth noting

Kimi K3's F-K3-01 (stale `CLAIM-SINGLE-MATERIALIZATION` tag in pass-22 historical section) is a genuinely new observation that no prior seat surfaced across 36+ rounds. It confirms the doc pipeline retains historical claim references verbatim — the intended traceability behavior — but leaves rename residue. Not a defect; queue for housekeeping.

Fable 5's F-FB-3 (mixed footer semantics: rolling vs frozen across historical sections) illuminates an under-specified aspect of the doc-generator. The pipeline currently rolls the "current corpus" footer into recent sections (pass-34, pass-35, pass-36) but leaves older sections frozen at their own-round counts. Either behavior is defensible; specifying which is intended would remove ambiguity for future auditors.

GPT 5.6 Sol's successful production after four corruptions is itself the significant fact of this round. The tight step-budget-with-forced-write protocol worked. Future OpenAI seats should get similar objectives — short, sequenced, with explicit save-before-response requirements — until the corruption mode is understood at the model level.
