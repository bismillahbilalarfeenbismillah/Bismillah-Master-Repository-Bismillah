## 0. What v0.5 is

v0.5 is a controlled, **append-only methodological extension** of the ratified SHUDP v0.4 baseline.
It integrates the newly published companion paper, "Is Evidence Independence an Axiom? Decomposing a
Proposed Extension to AX-SH" (Bilal Syed Arfeen, v1.0, July 24, 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21539074),
by adding a clearly labeled Axiom 5 (evidence-independence) layer on top of the unchanged v0.4
analysis. **It is not a rewrite of the incident record.** The July 22, 2026 incident-evidence freeze
and every incident fact are preserved. v0.4 correctly applied the four-axiom canon then available;
v0.5 adds A5 only after the companion paper's publication. No v0.5 passage claims v0.4 applied A5.
All v0.5 changes are identified collectively by the paper's Version and Provenance Note and are
individually mapped in this memorandum. Newly added analytic sections and passages carry an explicit
`[v0.5 post-freeze addition]` label in the source where a label fits cleanly (13 such labels);
short edits, notably the abstract and the contribution wording, are covered by the Version and
Provenance Note and by this memorandum rather than by an inline label. There is no claim that every
substantive insertion carries a literal inline tag.

## 1. Formal interpretation carried into v0.5 (from the companion paper)

The v0.5 A5 material preserves the companion paper's decomposition exactly:

- **Only A5.1 survives as Axiom 5:** acting-system-independent evidence generation, defined through
  the control predicate WithinAuth (S cannot cause, modify, suppress, reconfigure, or credential the
  evidence-generation act without an independently detectable violation).
- **A5.2 causal gating is NOT Axiom 5.** It is a proposed SHIELD/control-layer principle.
- **A5.4 operator independence is NOT Axiom 5.** It is a measurable deployment condition.
- **A5.3a (no-default) and A5.5 (boundary declaration) are theorems;** A5.3b (recording/type/
  escalation) is an open operational requirement.
- **A5 is not a fifth projection or fifth object** in SHUDP's four-way surface/substrate/outcome/
  projection distinction; it is an existential requirement over evidence generation.
- **Axiom-level status and event-level verdict are distinct:** absent timely independent evidence for
  a covered in-boundary occurrence, A5 is UNSATISFIED/nonconforming at the axiom level, while the
  event-level adjudicative verdict remains UNKNOWN.
