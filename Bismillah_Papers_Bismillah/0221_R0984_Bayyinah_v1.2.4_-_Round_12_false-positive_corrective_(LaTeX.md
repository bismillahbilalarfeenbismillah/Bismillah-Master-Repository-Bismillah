**CRITICAL: parser-form check before implementing the fingerprint.** ToUnicode CMaps in PDFs are stored as hex codepoint pairs (`<00> <0060>`, `<17> <200C>`, etc.). The fingerprint MUST match against whatever form `_parse_tounicode_cmap` returns. Two forms are possible:
- **(a)** raw hex codepoint sequences as strings: `'0060'`, `'00B4'`, `'200C'`, `'00660069'` (multi-codepoint targets like ligatures concatenate hex)
- **(b)** decoded Unicode strings: `` '`' ``, `'´'`, `'‌'`, `'fi'`

def is_canonical_ot1_or_t1(bfchar_entries: dict[int, str]) -> bool:
