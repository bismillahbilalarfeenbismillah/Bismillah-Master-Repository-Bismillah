## 3. Register absorbed from Grok's external review of the architecture

- **G-1 CONFIRMED and answered.** The transfer from theological categories to engineering controls is
  largely analogical, and the four transferred properties can be derived from ordinary
  incident-response and error-correction practice. Disposition: not deflected. Section 9 of the paper
  is new and exists to answer this, stating what does and does not survive removal of the theological
  frame. The honest position recorded there is that the theology carries the paper's normative
  structure and not its engineering recommendations. Falsification F-5 makes the residual testable.
- **G-2 CONFIRMED.** The paper does not show that propagation metrics are in practice more
  determinate than alignment metrics. Disposition: retained as an open empirical claim and named at
  F-3.
- **G-3 CONFIRMED and absorbed into Limitations.** In the current environment the urgent problems are
  classical: discovery outpacing patch capacity, autonomous exploitation, dual use. A theological
  account of transmission weight does not increase patch velocity. Section 11 now says this in the
  paper's own voice.
- **G-4 CONFIRMED.** Scholarly review is not optional. Disposition: promoted from a review-plan item
  to the paper's front matter, with five specific items enumerated so a scholar can answer them.
- **G-5 scope warning: "the direction is sound, the risk is diffusion, keep the spine tight."**
  Disposition: the paper is 6,538 words against an architecture of 5,097, rather than the 9,000-plus
  a full expansion would have produced. Sections were compressed, not added.
