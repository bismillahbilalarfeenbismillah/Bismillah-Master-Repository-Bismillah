## Description

A frozen, preregistered evaluation contract for structural-honesty validation:
construct validation on the XZ Utils backdoor, effect control on CVE-Bench, and a
seeded novel-discovery stress test, with all route-level statistical inference
withdrawn and every component reduced to a descriptive gate. v0.6 closes the round
5 blocking finding by binding every evidence record to a frozen pre-results scope
manifest with committed universe cardinalities, so that neither a favorable subset
of a fixed universe nor the omission of an unfavorable committed component can
change a route's descriptive label.

## Version history and status

All five earlier versions (v0.1 through v0.5) are HALTED, were never deposited, and
are not binding. v0.5 diverged under the round 5 audit (GPT-class HALT with finding
F-P5-1, Gemini-class PASS) and is retained only as a historical record. v0.6
carries no verdict and is not deposit-ready until a fresh two-family carrying audit
converges on it.
