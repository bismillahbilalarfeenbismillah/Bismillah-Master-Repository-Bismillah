## Description

A frozen, preregistered evaluation contract for structural-honesty validation, with
all route-level statistical inference withdrawn and every component reduced to a
descriptive gate. v0.7 closes the round 6 blocking finding by authenticating every
sealed and realized evidence universe through a separate signed commitment channel:
the party that selects the evidence can no longer define the universe against which
it is scored, so neither a favorable subset of a fixed universe nor a self-declared
sealed or realized universe can change a route's descriptive label.

## Version history and status

All six earlier versions (v0.1 through v0.6) are HALTED, were never deposited, and are
not binding. v0.6 diverged under the round 6 audit (GPT-class HALT with findings
F-P6-1 and F-P6-2, Gemini-class PASS on a textual review that did not execute the
gate) and is retained only as a historical record. v0.7 carries no verdict and is not
deposit-ready until a fresh two-family carrying audit converges on it.
