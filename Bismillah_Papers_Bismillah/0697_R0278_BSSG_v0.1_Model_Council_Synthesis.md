## 4. Comprehensive Analysis

**High-confidence findings.** All four seats converge on the shape of the object. The five-conjunct kernel is a sound fail-closed conjunction, and none of the seats proposed replacing it; the disagreements are about what surrounds it, not about the equation. Claude Opus 5 and GPT 5.6 Sol both formalize why: the kernel is a decidable admission function whose enforceable content is a safety property, and a single-trace monitor can enforce exactly that class. Convergence here is meaningful because the seats reached it from different directions. Claude worked from the trace-property and hyperproperty classification, GPT from three-valued predicate semantics and linearization, Gemini from a deterministic confinement perimeter, and GLM from adversarial reachability. Four independent routes to the same conclusion about the kernel's soundness is the strongest signal in the entire council, and it is why the draft freezes the equation and adds only a protocol obligation around it.

The second high-confidence convergence is negative, and it is the one that shaped the draft most. Three of the four seats independently concluded that three of the six advertised security properties cannot be claimed as the charter originally worded them. Non-bypassability is conditional on complete mediation, which is an assumption about system construction that can only ever be refuted. Non-equivocation is detection only for a single-trace monitor absent a trusted monotonic primitive; Claude cited the attested-memory and trusted-counter literature, GPT cited transparency-log checkpoint comparison, and GLM cited the need for serial evaluation or consensus, but all three land on the same restriction. Evidence independence is an assumption over a control relation, and both Claude and GPT produced the same counterexample independently: cryptographic separation without administrative separation is not independence. GLM reached it from the axiom side, noting that AX-SH 5 explicitly establishes non-derivability. When four models with different training lineages independently refuse to certify the same three claims, the correct producer response is to demote the claims, which is what Section 22 of the draft does through explicit claim levels.

The third convergence is that a decision at decision time does not bind an effect at execution time. Every seat raised time-of-check to time-of-use, and GPT supplied the most complete repair: a single immutable snapshot at decision, conditional redemption against current epochs at commit, and an explicit point of no return. This is the one place where the council added architecture rather than subtracting claims, and the draft adopts it as the atomic commit protocol, deliberately expressed as a protocol obligation recorded in the execution receipt rather than as a sixth conjunct, because the equation is frozen.

**Areas of divergence.** The sharpest split is over strict narrowing. Claude Opus 5 argues that monotone narrowing without a strictness requirement permits a legal but useless implementation: a ceiling identical at HEALTHY and DEGRADED satisfies the letter of monotonicity while narrowing nothing. GPT 5.6 Sol rejects universal strictness on the ground that it is impossible to satisfy when authority is already empty or when the degraded witness is irrelevant to the capability in hand, and proposes global non-increase plus strict removal of every capability whose assurance dependency failed. These are not reconcilable by wording; they encode different failure priors. Claude is defending against a lazy implementer, GPT against an over-specified invariant that a correct implementer cannot satisfy. The draft adopts strictness conditioned on a non-empty prior ceiling, which handles Claude's vacuity attack and GPT's empty-authority objection but not GPT's irrelevant-witness objection, and the residue is carried openly as OD-6 rather than resolved by fiat.

The second real divergence is over non-advantage of concealment, and here Gemini 3.1 Pro stands apart from the other three. Gemini treats the property as satisfied by construction, since omission maps to UNKNOWN and UNKNOWN cannot satisfy a conjunct. Claude and GPT both reject that reading as either vacuous or as a disguised effectiveness claim, with Claude proposing the two-clause architectural form and GPT proposing decision monotonicity under information loss. GLM decomposes the property into four sub-properties with different reachability. The draft adopts a merged form, Claude's structure with GPT's mechanically testable clause, and it preserves the disagreement by noting that Gemini's reading is exactly the vacuous half. Notably, Gemini is also the seat that supplied the concrete counterexample to its own position: the timing differential between denial classes leaks contract contents to a probing agent. That observation is the reason the draft carries OD-11 as blocking-adjacent, since the specification's own diagnostic richness is a regime signal.

The third divergence is about profiles. GPT holds that a Minimal profile that makes evidence independence optional certifies a logger rather than a gate. Claude and Gemini both accept a weaker Minimal with mandatory disclosure. GLM sidesteps by putting independence at the top level only. The draft follows Claude's disclosure route, which is a producer choice against the strongest objection in the council, and it is recorded as such rather than presented as consensus.

**Unique insights worth noting.** Two unique findings changed the artifact more than any agreement did. Claude Opus 5's discovery that the constraining deposit contains no implementation of the kernel anywhere is the reason the draft's lineage section carries an explicit prohibition against citing SHVVP's suite counts as enforcement engineering; without that audit, the draft would plausibly have inherited a category error. Claude's second contribution, encoding known impossibilities as passing tests, is now a structural feature of the test suite: the colluding-witness test asserts that the gate allows, so that any future claim of detection requires deleting a test, which is a visible act. GLM 5.2's honest-quorum problem is the third: it names the boundary at which the architecture stops, and it is the only finding in the council that no amount of specification work can move.

**Recommendations.** Treat the two HALT verdicts as unresolved, not as procedural noise: the draft should remain DRAFT and uncarried until a second independent implementation reproduces the frozen vectors, a cross-session monotonicity root is named, the canonicalization fork is closed, and the diagnostic-versus-concealment tension is resolved. Where seats disagreed, prefer publishing both positions with the producer's choice marked as a choice, since the disagreements here track genuine design forks rather than errors. Do not let the six-schema constraint be read as a claim that six is the right decomposition; three seats proposed different sets and the loss of a separate contract schema is real. Finally, resist any temptation to describe the council as approving anything: two seats halted, two approved conditionally, and none of the conditions has been discharged.
