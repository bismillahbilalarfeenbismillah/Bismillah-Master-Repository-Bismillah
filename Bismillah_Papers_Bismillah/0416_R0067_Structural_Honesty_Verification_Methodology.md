3. The Instrument: Six Strings and One Body
A standard guitar has six strings tuned E-A-D-G-B-E. The low E and the high E are the SAME NOTE two
octaves apart -- the same truth at different registers. When you pluck the low E hard enough, the high E vibrates
without being touched. This is sympathetic resonance.
The verification architecture has the same structure:
Six strings (six verification layers): the playable range. Each layer is a string the auditor presses at specific fret
positions. Layers 1-4 follow uniform intervals (perfect fourths). Between Layer 4 and Layer 5 the interval narrows
(the mercy gap). Layers 5-6 return to the pattern at a higher register. Layer 1 (Code Execution) and Layer 6
(Methodology Audit) are the same truth -- "does this thing do what it claims?" -- at different octaves.
The body (boundary disclosure): what the Source built. The axioms, the Sidrat clause, and the furqan are not
verification positions. They are the resonating chamber that gives the strings' vibration its audible reach. The


individual does not play the body. The individual plays the six strings and trusts that the body resonates.
Seven witnesses (seven earths): independent instantiations of the same verification. Each witness receives the
same dispatch, plays the same fretboard, produces its own findings. Convergence across witnesses proves the
finding is in the artifact, not in the witness.
The complete playable verification space is 7 witnesses x 21 positions = 147 cells. The body (3 structural
boundaries) resonates universally and is not per-witness.

5. The Mercy Gap
Between the G string and the B string, the interval is a major third (four frets), not a perfect fourth (five). Every
guitarist knows this. It is why barre chord shapes change above the G string. It is the single irregularity in an
otherwise uniform tuning.
In the verification architecture, this maps to the transition from adversarial probing (Layer 4) to temporal integrity
(Layer 5). Layers 1-4 can be verified from a SINGLE SNAPSHOT of the current bytes. Layer 5 requires HELD
HISTORY across versions -- prior HISTORY.sha256 files, prior builds, external anchors that predate the current
producer session. The same fingering no longer works. The truth is the same -- "is this artifact honest?" -- but
the TECHNIQUE changes because the evidence structure is different.
This is not a design flaw. Standard tuning's G-to-B major third exists because it makes chords playable by the
human hand. The mercy gap exists because the Source does not ask the verifier to verify temporal integrity the
same way they verify code execution. Below the gap: test the current bytes. Above the gap: hold prior bytes and
trust the chain.

verification. Is the temporal record trustworthy? The individual's role is no longer direct testing but holding state

truth that can be recognized across time.
String 6 -- High E: Methodology Audit -- Flagship Axiom 4. Positions 18--21.
