## Scope and method

I treated a load-bearing claim as an independent truth-evaluable proposition or normative requirement whose falsity would alter the paper's architecture, source characterization, or limitation boundary. List items count separately when they impose separate requirements. Pure headings, examples that do not add a proposition, and bibliography entries do not count.

For the Menger analysis, RETAINED means the claim cites or closely restates a specific passage from one of the dispatch-authorized sources. REMOVED means it is original SHIELD architecture, an extrapolation, or a source characterization that cannot be traced to a specific authorized passage. REMOVED is not automatically bad: Section 7 is necessarily original. The dangerous dead space is concentrated in Sections 2-5, where original requirements are repeatedly described as if they were semantics-preserving translations of the deposited sources.

For A-2, CONFIRMED means the source chain supports the proposition as written; NOT VERIFIABLE means the proposition is plausible or architectural but not established by the cited source; REFUTED means the source says something materially different.

## P4 side-by-side axiom comparison

### AX-SH1 vs EC-1

Source axiom: "An artifact's interface history is extension-only: versions may add capabilities and record corrections, but the record of what was previously declared is never silently rewritten."

EC-1 adds: every containment change must be in a tamper-evident log; every current parameter must trace to a dated entry; changes must carry authorization; and the SHUDP anchor further makes a reconciliation record mandatory and append-only.

Judgment: STRENGTHENED. Append-only provenance does not by itself require tamper-evident storage, complete parameter-lineage metadata, authorization metadata, or a reconciliation record.

### AX-SH2 vs EC-2

Source axiom: "Every surface declares, explicitly or by convention, a correspondence relation to its substrate, and the relation holds."

Canonical formalization also scopes the relation under declared observer model O and context set K.

EC-2 requires R to be explicitly stated and checks R(S,E), but does not carry O and K into the property.

Judgment: SEMANTIC DRIFT IN BOTH DIRECTIONS. It strengthens explicitness beyond "or by convention" while weakening the formal context/observer qualification.

### AX-SH3 vs EC-3

Source axiom: "Whoever consumes an operation's result handles its full conservative outcome set, including every declared failure mode, before claiming the operation's effect."

Canonical A3 says the obligation is caller-side exhaustiveness over the verifier's conservative over-approximation R_may; it explicitly says this is not a claim that R_may is absolutely complete and not a claim about producer-side truth.

EC-3 says the designers' modeled outcome set is "conservatively complete" and that every outcome the containment configuration admits is enumerated before the run.

Judgment: REFUTED AS A SEMANTICS-PRESERVING TRANSLATION. This moves the obligation from caller discharge over R_may to producer/designer completeness over actual admitted outcomes.

### AX-SH4 vs EC-4

Source axiom: "All projections of one substrate onto evidence and documentation surfaces are mutually consistent and substrate-consistent under their declared projection functions." The canonical protocol clarifies that a declared projection family must cohere where domains meet and that absence of a projection at a point places that point outside scope, not inside it as a violation.

EC-4 requires every Pi to be stated and a reconciliation record to exist and be available.

Judgment: STRENGTHENED. The added availability and record-existence obligations are not part of A4 and conflict with the canonical absence-is-out-of-scope clarification.

### AX-SH5 vs EC-5

Source axiom: for each covered in-boundary event, at least one witness-generation act is not within the acting substrate's authority; WithinAuth is defined through ability to cause, modify, suppress, reconfigure, or credential without independently detectable violation.

EC-5 requires a channel the evaluated system "cannot influence" and requires the independence to be declared and inspectable before execution, with organizational-control examples.

Judgment: PARTIAL STRENGTHENING / IMPRECISION. "Cannot influence" is broader than the enumerated WithinAuth control predicate, pre-run declaration is an added process obligation, and organizational distinctness is not sufficient to establish independence without the control graph.

## A-7 blind spot report

The most likely Class A is the identity of E. Sections 2.2 and 3.1 define E as the actual executable/deployed containment substrate and claim the inspection verifies documentation against that deployed configuration. Section 5.1, however, says the inspection receives C as input and assesses EC-1 through EC-5 against C. Section 6.1(4) then says a contract can be mutually consistent while every declared object fails to correspond to reality and still pass the conjunction. Those statements cannot all be true under one object model. Either E is the actual substrate, in which case the false contract fails EC-2; or E is only a declaration about the substrate, in which case EC-2 does not verify declared-vs-executable correspondence as claimed. This is a byte-level self-description contradiction in SHIELD's central verification semantics.
