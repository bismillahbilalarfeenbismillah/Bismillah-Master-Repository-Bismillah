6. The Maqam (Emergent Analytical Character)
In Arabic music, a maqam is a melodic mode -- a selection of notes from the 24 quarter
tones that gives a piece its character. The maqam is not the instrument. It is what the
performer PLAYS on the instrument.
In the audit program, each seat's model architecture produces findings with a
characteristic pattern -- a natural emphasis on certain frets, a natural insensitivity to
others. This is the seat's maqam. It emerges; it is not assigned.
When the triage hears the same maqam from two different strings (two seats emphasizing
the same fret region independently), this is RESONANCE. When two strings play different
maqams that share a common note at the same fret, this is CONVERGENCE. Both are
informative. Resonance confirms the string's tuning. Convergence confirms the note's
truth.
