## What this is
A frozen preregistration instrument for a cyber-agent validation program: the
evaluation contract plus an executable, adversarially-tested route gate that fixes the
falsifiers, thresholds, decision rules, and universe-commitment machinery in advance of
any corpus, pilot, or session. It is CARRIED (independently verified by two executing
non-producer model families of different vendors) and deposited so the freeze is
externally witnessed before its data exists.
