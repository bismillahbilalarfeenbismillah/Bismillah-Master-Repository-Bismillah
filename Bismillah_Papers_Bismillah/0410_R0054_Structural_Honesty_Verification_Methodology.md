The layers are nested -- each encompasses everything below it. A failure at any layer blocks everything above. Verification cost increases with depth. Each layer is governed by a specific paper in the program chain and carries a PMD Tier classification.

Governed by: CI v0.3.2 specification. Tier: 1. Cost: seconds.

Title, Status, Section 0.1, Section 22, entry -- all surfaces must agree. Does the artifact cohere across its own faces?

### Layer 4: Adversarial Probing (Fourth Heaven)

Mutation probes, false-PASS discovery, sabotage with resealed sums. Does the verification actually discriminate?

Governed by: Flagship Axiom 3 (caller-side exhaustiveness over conservative outcome sets). Tier: 1 for individual probes (deterministic), Tier 2 for coverage claims (completeness unproven). Cost: minutes to hours.

### Layer 5: Temporal Integrity (Fifth Heaven)

HISTORY.sha256, PRIOR_HISTORY cross-anchoring, append-only verification against held bytes. Is the temporal record trustworthy?

Governed by: Flagship Axiom 1 (append-only provenance). Tier: 1 for seal verification, Tier 2 for cross-version comparison (requires held prior bytes). Cost: requires continuous seats.

### Layer 6: Methodology Audit (Sixth Heaven)

The dispatch structure, triage rules, finding format, convergence definitions, and tagging discipline. Does the audit methodology faithfully transmit what the artifact contains?

Governed by: Flagship Axiom 4 (cross-projection consistency of evidence and documentation surfaces). Tier: 2 (structurally direct but coverage unproven). Cost: a full session.

### Layer 7: Boundary Disclosure (Seventh Heaven)

The axiom set, the Goedel sentence, the sufficiency criterion. What the system submits rather than verifies.

Governed by: A5 Paper (evidence independence: "at least one evidence record from outside the acting substrate's authority"). Tier: 3 for the Goedel analogy (structural correspondence), Tier 1 for axiom statements (each axiom is stated, not derived), outside all tiers for the sufficiency criterion (it is the meta-criterion by which tiers are judged). Cost: the entire program.

Above Layer 7: Sidrat al-Muntaha (the Lote Tree). Beyond it, no verification passes. Kursi (the verification envelope). 'Arsh (the Throne, the sufficiency judgment). al-Lawh al-Mahfuz (the preregistration). al-Qalam (the producer).

## 4. The Seven Earths of Independent Witness

The earths are not layers and not domains. They are SEATS -- distinct instantiations of the same verification. Each earth receives the same dispatch, encounters the same artifact, and produces its own findings. Their convergence proves the finding is in the artifact, not in the seat.

### The Mutawatir Principle

From hadith science (SHV Method v1.1, Section 3): a report carried by so many independent chains that their agreement on an error is implausible is treated as effectively certain. Not logically certain -- the possibility of shared error remains (this is Axiom A-X3, model non-collusion). But evidentially as strong as human knowledge gets.

Each seat is a chain. Each seat's finding is its own -- not copied, not influenced by another seat's report (fresh instances), not a duplicate. Convergence of N independent seats on the same finding is N-chain mutawatir evidence.

### Finding Classification

Each finding carries:

- **Layer** (L1--L7): where in the verification depth hierarchy it lives.

Convergence >= 2 under the same dispatch is the operational equivalent of mutawatir. Convergence = 1 is ahad (single-chain) -- the finding stands but carries less evidentiary weight for the triage.

## 7. The Barzakh Gate

Between a finding's production (seat report) and its entry into the triage (resurrection as a convergent group), the finding passes through the Barzakh gate. Three questions, operationalized from the Munkar-Nakir tradition and grounded in SHV Method v1.1 disciplines D1--D8.

### Question 1: "Who is your Lord?" (Authority)

SHV disciplines D1 (substrate over attestation) and D6 (producer-quarantined convergence).

- **Righteous:** "I speak from certainty. Here is the tool command and its verbatim output." Evidence is Tier 1 -- independently re-executable.
- **Wicked:** "I speak from guesswork. The code appears incorrect." No tool output. Evidence is assertion.

Threshold: findings without execution evidence are flagged ASSERTED and carry reduced convergence weight.

### Question 2: "What is your religion?" (Methodology)

SHV disciplines D2 (chain and content separate), D4 (name the thesis), D5 (verify at every scale).

- **Righteous:** "My way is tested. It holds up under examination." The finding cites a specific layer (L1--L7) and the evidence meets that layer's Tier standard.
- **Wicked:** "My way is untested. I assumed it was true." The finding invokes a framework without applying its methodology.

Threshold: findings whose evidence does not meet their claimed layer's Tier standard are reclassified or returned.

### Question 3: "Where does this come from?" (Lineage)

SHV disciplines D3 (hunt the hidden defect), D7 (hold honesty over time).

- **Righteous:** "I can trace it back, hand to hand, to its source." The finding cites its prior instance (if recurrent) and explains why the prior repair failed.
- **Wicked:** "It came from nowhere. A new claim with no lineage." The finding claims novelty but is actually a recurrence of a prior defect marked CLOSED.

Threshold: recurrent findings that do not cite lineage are returned for tracing. Findings with false lineage are flagged.

Findings that fail all three questions are NOTED as unverifiable and set aside. They do not enter the convergence count.
