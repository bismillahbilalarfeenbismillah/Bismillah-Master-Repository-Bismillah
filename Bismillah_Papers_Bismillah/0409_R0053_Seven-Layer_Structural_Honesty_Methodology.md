## 7. The Barzakh Gate (Finding Validation)

Between a finding's production (in the seat report) and its entry into the triage (the resurrection as a convergent group), the finding passes through the Barzakh gate. Three questions, operationalized from the Munkar-Nakir tradition:

### Question 1: "Who is your Lord?" -- Authority

What is the finding's evidence chain?

- **Righteous soul:** "I speak from certainty, with evidence written and clear." The finding cites a tool command and its verbatim output. sha256sum returned X. build.py line N reads Y. The mutation was applied, the hash changed, the build passed (or failed).
- **Wicked soul:** "I speak from guesswork and appearance." The finding asserts "the code appears incorrect" without execution evidence.

Threshold: a finding without execution evidence is flagged ASSERTED and carries reduced weight in the convergence count.

### Question 2: "What is your religion?" -- Methodology

Under which analytical lens was this finding produced, and does the evidence meet that lens's standard?

- **Righteous soul:** "My way is tested. It holds up when examined." A GAN-convergence finding demonstrates that the generator's output is invariant to a mutation that should change it (the discriminator failed to discriminate). A Structural Revelation finding shows a specific REV-layer violation with the bytes at both layers cited.
- **Wicked soul:** "My way is untested. I assumed it was true." The finding invokes a framework without applying its methodology.

Threshold: a finding whose evidence does not match its claimed framework is reclassified or returned to the seat for clarification.

### Question 3: "Where does this come from?" -- Lineage

Is this finding novel or recurrent? If recurrent, has the prior instance been discharged?

- **Righteous soul:** "I can trace it back, hand to hand, to its source." The finding cites its prior instance (e.g., "this is R19 Surface 2 migrated upward; the prior repair closed the value-level error but introduced a gate-claim error"). The lineage is the defect migration chain.
- **Wicked soul:** "It came from nowhere. A new claim with no lineage." The finding claims novelty but is actually a recurrence of a prior defect that was marked CLOSED. (This is the phantom-repair class -- R7/v0.48 recurrence.)

Threshold: a recurrent finding that does not cite its lineage is returned for lineage tracing. A finding with false lineage (claiming prior instance X when X was a different surface) is flagged for correction.

## 12. The Four-Scribe Tagging Discipline
