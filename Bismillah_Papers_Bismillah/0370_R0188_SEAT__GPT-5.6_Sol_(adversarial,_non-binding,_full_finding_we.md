## Hurst R/S methodology and uncertainty

For each size n=4,...,18, the series is partitioned from v0.1 into non-overlapping blocks of length n; the remainder is discarded. In each block, deviations from the block mean are cumulatively summed; R is max minus min of that cumulative series; S is the sample standard deviation (ddof=1); R/S is undefined and omitted for constant blocks. Arithmetic mean R/S is taken at each n, and H is the OLS slope of log(mean R/S) on log(n). All 15 sizes remained estimable, but only 1-3 nonconstant blocks informed many sizes. The null uses 2,000 independent Fisher-Yates shuffles with Python `random.Random(370056)`; seed=370056.
