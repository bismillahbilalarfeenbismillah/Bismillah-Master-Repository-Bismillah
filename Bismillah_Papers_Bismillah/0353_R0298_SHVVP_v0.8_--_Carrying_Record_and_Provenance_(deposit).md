## Reference keys, not production keys

The three authority keys frozen in this instrument are REFERENCE keys, sufficient to fix
and prove the gate's logic (which the two families verified). They bind no live universe,
because no corpus, pilot, or session exists yet: this is a preregistration frozen before
its data. When the validation program actually executes (Phase A onward), the operator
installs the production authority keys held by the real custodians; that changes
`MANIFEST_SHA256` and is a versioned, freshly-carried step at that time. No private key of
any kind is included in this deposit.
