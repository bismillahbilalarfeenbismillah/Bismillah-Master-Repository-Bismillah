## 3. Part 2 — The convergence question

> Is three-seat adversarial convergence across V32–V34 (GPT-5.6, GLM-5.2, Kimi K2.6) — zero-BLOCKER, three vendor families, all non-producer — sufficient to accept convergence, when the two-family carry attempt (DeepSeek, Qwen) failed due to platform execution limitations, not artifact defects?

### Answer: QUALIFIED-YES — advisory, non-load-bearing

**Accept *convergence*. Do not let convergence discharge the *carry*.** These are two different gates, and the record supports the first while leaving the second undischarged. The artifact's own banner — DRAFT / NOT CARRIED — is exactly the honest label and should be retained until a genuine executing carry succeeds.

### 3.1 Convergence is genuinely achieved under PMD §0.4

PMD v1.2 §0.4 (`PMD_v1_2_BAF_v3_0.md`) defines convergence as: *"Two consecutive cross-vendor rounds produce 0 CRITICAL findings"*, with HIGH residual 0 (or a signed exception), MEDIUM/LOW absorbed or explicitly non-operative, and standing-rule compliance passing. The record shows **three** consecutive cross-vendor rounds (V32–V34) at zero findings in the blocking band across three non-producer vendor families — exceeding the two-round bar. The residuals (GPT's per-round MODERATE, all documentation staleness) map to the MEDIUM band of the severity rubric, whose disposition is explicitly *"ship-permissive"* into next-version absorption (`bayyinah_severity-rubric.md`, MEDIUM disposition), and MEDIUM does not enter the §0.4 convergence predicate at all. So on the brief's angle 4: **MODERATE/MEDIUM-only residuals do not defeat convergence** — provided they are documented in the next-version appendix as the rubric requires, which the missing `REPAIR_STATUS` V33/V34 blocks currently do *not* demonstrate on shipped bytes (see F-CF-3).

On angle 1 (does three exceed the bar, or is fresh rotation independently required): PMD §0.4 itself does not demand fresh rotation. The fresh-rotation clause lives in SHV D6 (`SHV_Method_v1_1.pdf`, D6): certify *"only when independent checkers agree repeatedly, **ideally** across two consecutive clean rounds with at least one checker rotated in fresh, so that agreement is not an artifact of a fixed panel."* The word is "ideally" — a strengthening condition, not a hard conjunct. V32–V34 satisfies D6's core (repeated agreement, multi-family, producer-quarantined, findings re-derived from primary bytes) but **fails its ideal form: the panel was fixed across all three clean rounds**. The third consecutive round adds real value (it further defeats the luck hypothesis) but adds **zero** value against the fixed-panel failure mode D6 names, because it is the same panel. Three-of-same is not a substitute for one-fresh. The intended fresh rotation *was* the carry pool — and it failed.

### 3.2 The carry is a separate load-bearing gate; convergence cannot substitute for it

Three independent lines of methodology force this, and the producer's own frozen record concedes it:

1. **SHVVP §0.3** (`SHVVP_v0_8_flagship.pdf`) is direct precedent: after v0.5's withdrawal, the frozen consequence was *"v0.5 has NOT been carried. It requires a fresh carrying audit by two non-producer seats of different vendor families ... before deposit. Producer-lineage, editor-lineage and chair review of v0.5 is ADVISORY and NON-CARRYING. No claim of audit convergence attaches to v0.5 at the time of this freeze."* The carrying audit is a **deposit gate**, distinct from round-level convergence, and no volume of non-carrying review substitutes. The BSSG program's own ledger applies the same rule to itself: *"All three are byte-authoritative continuous/one-shot seats and **non-carrying**; a fresh different-vendor rotation remains owed before any carry"* (`REPAIR_STATUS.md`, V29 adjudication banner, lines 124–125). The three convergence seats are, by the program's own frozen text, structurally non-carrying — not because of vendor identity but because they are the incumbent panel.

2. **SHUDP Axiom 5** (`SHUDP_v0_5_flagship.pdf`, "Axiom 5, evidence independence"): for each covered event there must exist an evidence record whose generation is *"not within S's authority"* (WithinAuth). The carry is precisely BSSG's A5 instrument — execution evidence generated outside the producer's and the incumbent panel's authority. The DeepSeek result is the canonical A5 anti-pattern: its "CLEAN" restated embedded results ("Simulated from Embedded Test Results", placeholder identity values) — in SHUDP's own words, *"a candidate witness is not a discharged existential requirement."* The Qwen result generated real execution evidence but against non-canonical bytes (SHA mismatches), so the evidence does not attach to the artifact. SHUDP also supplies the disciplined verdict vocabulary: the carry is **NOT EVALUABLE / UNDISCHARGED, not FAILED** — *"NOT EVALUABLE, not FAILED, is the disciplined verdict"* — and equally, nothing about the failure discharges the requirement.

3. **SHVVP §8.0** (`SHVVP_v0_8_flagship.pdf`) supplies the governing lesson for what to do when a required demonstration cannot be produced: v0.5 did not invent a third inferential mechanism to rescue the route claim; it **withdrew the claim** — *"a sampling model invented to rescue a claim is exactly the postdiction that preregistration exists to prevent"*, and *"Removing an unjustified inferential claim strengthens the instrument."* The analog here: do not construct a post-hoc argument that three-family convergence "counts as" a two-family carry. Narrow the claim to what the record honestly supports — **converged, NOT CARRIED** — which is what the shipped banner already says.

### 3.3 The specific sub-questions

- **Angle 2 (capability-shortfall: methodological blocker or substrate limitation?)** Substrate limitation. Under `bayyinah_audit-modes.md`, Mode A capability is a property of the execution substrate, not the artifact ("Requires git / tool access; not always available"), and the rotation doc's tier table records Mode A capability per vendor as an infrastructure fact. Neither carry failure produced an artifact finding; Qwen's ISSUES-FOUND was a delivery-integrity report, honestly filed. Therefore the failed carry does **not** invalidate the V32–V34 convergence, does **not** constitute adverse evidence against the artifact, and does **not** HALT the chain. But a substrate limitation excuses the *attempt*, not the *requirement*: SHVVP §0.3's deposit gate is stated over outcomes ("requires a fresh carrying audit ... before deposit"), not over efforts.
- **Angle 3 (does three-family convergence substitute for the carry?)** No, per §3.2. The carry's function under SHV D6 and SHUDP A5 is *fresh, executing, panel-independent* evidence. The three seats are neither fresh nor carry-eligible under the program's own frozen non-carrying designation (`REPAIR_STATUS.md` V29 banner).
- **Angle 4 (MODERATE-only residuals):** Below threshold; do not defeat convergence (PMD §0.4; `bayyinah_severity-rubric.md` MEDIUM disposition). See §3.1.
- **Angle 5 (GLM K=5 over-coverage):** **Strengthens the convergence record, does not advance the carry.** The 19607-schedule figure is exactly Σ7^i (i=1..5) under the shipped enumerator — generative evidence of genuine execution beyond shipped coverage. But GLM is an incumbent seat; deeper verification by an incumbent addresses assurance depth, not panel independence, which is the property the carry exists to supply (SHV D6 fixed-panel clause).
- **PMD M1.5 / mechanical-traceability exception** (PMD v1.2 §0.5 item 5, `PMD_v1_2_BAF_v3_0.md`; restated as the same-instance §0.1 named exception in `bayyinah_cross-vendor-rotation.md`): **does not apply, and this matters.** The exception waives *cross-instance re-audit of an absorption cycle* when the cycle executed prescribed routes with zero architectural latitude, on project-lead signoff. It is scoped to absorption re-audits; it is not a waiver instrument for the deposit-time carry gate, and stretching it there would collide directly with SHVVP §0.3's frozen carry requirement and SHV D6's producer-quarantine. Where it *does* legitimately apply: the pending mechanical fixes for the documentation-staleness residuals (V33/V34 `REPAIR_STATUS`/README count refreshes) can be absorbed under M1.5 with project-lead signoff and without owing a fresh full round — those are disposition-prescribed routes with no latitude. (Note the citation-integrity caveat in F-CF-2: the label "M1.5" does not resolve cleanly in the shipped corpus.)

### 3.4 Operative recommendation (advisory)

1. **Accept convergence** for V32–V34 under PMD §0.4 — this is a non-producer-seat decision; I merely concur analytically.
2. **Retain DRAFT / NOT CARRIED** until two non-producer, different-vendor-family seats execute exact canonical bytes and return clean. The record itself suggests the viable substrate: the rotation doc lists Perplexity (tool-augmented) as a Tier 1, Mode A-strong, load-bearing substrate verifier (`bayyinah_cross-vendor-rotation.md`, Tier 1), and this very council cycle demonstrates byte-faithful sandbox delivery (SHA-verified) with real execution. A carry pool selected for **demonstrated Mode A capability** (rather than chat-only interfaces) cures the DeepSeek/Qwen failure mode without weakening the rule. Delivery must be by hash-verified archive, not text-paste extraction, to prevent recurrence of the Qwen byte-corruption mode.
3. Absorb the staleness residuals under M1.5 with project-lead signoff (mechanical routes only), and regenerate the full package so G1/G8/G9/G10 pass on the deposited bytes.

## 5. Dissenting angle

Even while concluding QUALIFIED-YES, I dissent from one element of the producer's framing: **the brief presents the carry failure as external bad luck ("platform limitations, not artifact defects") — but carry-pool selection is a producer-chain design decision, and this one was falsifiably poor.** The rotation doc's own tier table records Mode A capability per vendor as a known, documented property (`bayyinah_cross-vendor-rotation.md`, "Audit-modes by tier"); a carry rule whose entire purpose is *executing* exact bytes was pointed at a chat-only interface (DeepSeek) and a text-paste delivery channel (Qwen) — substrates predictably incapable of discharging it. Under SHV D6's falsification clause, a certification path that fails should trigger *strengthening the independence requirement*, not sympathy for the attempt. The honest description is not "the carry failed through no fault of the artifact" (true but incomplete) — it is "the producer chain selected a carry substrate that could not execute, so the carry obligation remains fully open and undiminished." The three-round convergence is real and creditable; the carry narrative in the brief drifts toward treating an unexecuted gate as morally satisfied. SHVVP §8.0's lesson cuts precisely here: withdraw or narrow the claim; never rescue it by reframing.
