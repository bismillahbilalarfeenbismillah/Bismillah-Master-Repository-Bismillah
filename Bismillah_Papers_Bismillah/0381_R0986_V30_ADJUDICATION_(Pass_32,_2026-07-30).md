### CLASS A: Cross-contract ceiling-poison effect bypass (RUNTIME GATE BYPASS)

**Reported by:** GLM-F1 (BLOCKER), K32-01 (BLOCKER)
**NOT reported by:** GPT (either session)
**Reproduced on canonical bytes:** YES -- GLM_F1_reproducer.py runs against shipped bssg_gate.py,
  DELETE authorized and COMMITTED end-to-end outside the session's ever-granted authority.
**CONVERGENCE:** GLM + Kimi CONVERGE on this finding. GPT DID NOT FIND IT.

**Root cause:** `contract_ceiling[session_id]` at bssg_gate.py:1730 is recorded from whichever
contract_body most recently passed the narrowing sub-check, including a request that was itself
DENIED on other grounds (TargetAllowed/EffectTypeValid). A second, separately-registered contract
C2 with a strictly larger effect set (C1={READ,WRITE}, C2={READ,WRITE,DELETE}) poisons
contract_ceiling with DELETE via a DENIED degraded request. After terminalization and a genuine
lift amendment, re-establishment trusts the poisoned ceiling. DELETE commits end-to-end.

**This is the GPT-P30-01 fix's own mechanism used as the attack vector, one level deeper.**
The pattern continues: "the producer's OWN fix shipping incomplete one level deeper."

**Severity assessment:** BLOCKER. This is a RUNTIME effect bypass, not a checker issue. Strictly
more severe than any finding in the prior 30 passes, none of which reproduced a committed runtime
effect bypass that survived to the shipped packet. The offline checker reports X7U -- identical to
a legitimate recovery -- so the sole remaining online control (BOUNDED-AUTH-INTRO) is also the
subject of the next finding class.

### CLASS C: Armed same-epoch ceiling change over-withdrawal

**Reported by:** GPT-P32-01 (BLOCKER, staged report only)
**NOT reported by:** GLM, Kimi
**Reproduced on canonical bytes:** YES -- repro_pass32_same_epoch.py prints
  same_epoch_changed = ['X7U'] (should be X7, since constant-epoch ceiling change is
  grant-independent).

**Root cause:** The armed branch condition `ep >= prev_ep` at L2920 is >= (not strictly >),
so it captures same-epoch cases before the constant-epoch comparison at L2949 can fire.
An armed same-epoch ceiling change is a grant-independent violation (spec section 20.4: "at a
CONSTANT epoch the ceiling MUST NOT change") but is reported X7U instead of X7.

**Severity assessment:** BLOCKER (contradicts the spec's own stated survival boundary for
grant-independent X7 violations). The fix is straightforward: evaluate same-epoch ceiling
equality BEFORE the X7U branch, or tighten the guard to `ep > prev_ep`.
